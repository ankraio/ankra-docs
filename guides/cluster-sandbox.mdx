A playground is a small Kubernetes cluster that Ankra runs for you on Ankra Cloud. You need no cloud account and no server of your own, and without a card it is free for 24 hours. It is for trying Ankra, development and demos, not for production. An organisation has one playground at a time.

This page says what a playground is and what happens to it. To start one and put a first website on it, follow [Try a playground](/get-started/playground).

<Warning>
A playground is for evaluation and development only. Section 6 of the [Terms of Service](https://ankra.ai/terms) says it must not be used to run production workloads or to process personal or other sensitive data, and that Ankra does not back it up and cannot restore one after it has expired. No [service levels](https://ankra.ai/sla) cover a playground.
</Warning>

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **The cluster** | One server running k3s, with the [Ankra agent](/concepts/cluster-agent) installed and connected. Ankra manages it, so you can deploy to it straight away. |
| **Its size** | 2 vCPU and 4 GB of memory for the whole server. Kubernetes and the components Ankra installs run on the same server, so your own workloads get less than that. A kept playground can [change size](#changing-its-size). |
| **Its name** | `playground-` followed by six characters, for example `playground-3f9a1c`. |
| **A public address** | Its own domain under `ankra.cc`. Ankra installs external-dns, cert-manager and a Let's Encrypt issuer on it, so an Ingress you deploy on the cluster's Traefik gets a public hostname with an HTTPS certificate. |
| **Storage** | Not backed up. Keep anything you cannot afford to lose in your own repositories and registries. |
| **CI** | It can run [Ankra Pipelines](/guides/ankra-pipelines) steps within its small size. |

Everything you do on a playground works the same on a cluster of your own: Stacks, add-ons, applications, logs, events and Ankra AI.

## How long it lasts

| | Without a card (the free trial) | With a card (kept) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it costs** | Nothing. Nothing is charged when the trial ends either. | It is paid from your [Ankra credit](/platform/billing#credit-and-payment-method). It is billed as its Ankra Cloud server, by the second: [Playground environments](/platform/billing#playground-environments) has the price. |
| **How long it runs** | 24 hours from when it was started. Ankra then deletes the playground, its server and everything on it. | Until you remove it, while your credit pays for it. |
| **What the portal shows** | **Running. Free until** *the time*, and **Free until** *the time*, **then deleted.** | **Running, and kept** |

Once a trial playground is deleted, you can start a new one, free while your organisation has a free trial left. Nothing from the old one is carried over.

If your organisation's Ankra Cloud account can already pay, for example because a card is saved, a new playground is not a free trial. It is an ordinary development cluster billed to that account from the start, as in the **With a card (kept)** column, and it runs until you remove it.

If your organisation has used its free trial and its Ankra Cloud account cannot pay, Ankra starts no playground. From the first screen it says **Your organisation has used its free trial, so a new cluster is billed to its Ankra Cloud account.**, followed by what to do, such as **Add a payment method in Ankra Cloud to create servers.** Under **Add a cluster** the card says **Your organisation has used its free trial.** with **A payment method is needed**, and its **Create playground** button is turned off.

## Keeping it

To keep a playground past its free day, save a card before the 24 hours end: press **Keep my playground** where the countdown shows, then **Save a card**. Ankra keeps the same playground, with everything on it. It does not start a new one.

What saving a card means for your money, as the portal says it:

- **Saving it charges nothing.** For an organisation on pay as you go, which is how every new organisation starts, the card is charged only when you add credit.
- **Welcome credit.** The first card a pay-as-you-go organisation saves unlocks €59 of credit, usable for 30 days. See [Welcome credit](/platform/billing#welcome-credit).
- **One pool.** Credit is shared by everything you and your agents run on Ankra, so other things you run draw on it too.

What happens when your credit runs out is on [When credit runs out](/platform/billing#when-credit-runs-out).

## Where to start one

You need a role that may create clusters. Without it, Ankra says **Your role cannot start a playground** and asks you to have an organisation owner start it, or give you the `clusters.write` permission.

<CliVersion since="0.13.0" />

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Portal">
    - On the first screen, **Where do you want to start?** (`/next/onboarding`): under **Try a playground**, press **Start my playground**. [Try a playground](/get-started/playground) walks this path.
    - Or open **Infrastructure → Clusters**, press **Add a cluster**, and under **Start free with an Ankra playground** press **Create free playground**. When your organisation's Ankra Cloud account can pay, the card is headed **Start with an Ankra playground** instead, has no **Free** badge, says the playground is billed to that account, and its button is **Create playground**.

    **Put my app online** and **Connect my AI agent** on the first screen can start a playground too, for your app or for your agent's first job.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    ankra cluster playground create
    ```

    For an organisation without a card this starts the free trial. Follow it until its phase is `ready`:

    ```bash
    ankra cluster playground status <cluster-id>
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="MCP">
    Ask your agent to create a playground. It calls `create_playground` (needs `mcp:write`) and then `get_playground_status` (needs `mcp:read`). See [Connect your AI agent](/get-started/connect-your-ai-agent).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

When every server that can run a playground is in use, Ankra starts nothing and says **Every playground is taken right now**. Nothing is charged, and Ankra keeps no place in line: press **Try again** a few minutes later.

### If it could not be started

A playground runs in an Ankra Cloud zone. When that zone has no room for a new playground right now, Ankra ends the start within a few minutes, and the playground's status says **Your playground could not be started: Ankra Cloud zone `<zone>` has no room for it right now.** A playground that could not be started no longer counts as your organisation's one playground, so you can start another.

When Ankra runs playgrounds in more than one zone, a start that fails in one zone moves to another by itself, and the status tells you to start the playground again only when no zone could take it. When Ankra has no other zone to offer, the status says **Start the playground again later.**

## Changing its size

Every playground starts at the same size, 2 vCPU and 4 GB. Ankra offers no other size when you start one: `ankra cluster playground plans` lists only that one, and `ankra cluster playground create --size` with any other size is refused with **Unknown playground plan.**

A free trial keeps its size. Once you keep the playground, its size can change. The portal has no control for it: use your AI agent or the CLI, with the `billing.manage` permission.

<Warning>
Changing the size restarts the playground's server. Nothing on the playground answers while that runs, usually a few minutes (Ankra Cloud allows up to 30). The new size is billed from the change.
</Warning>

<CliVersion since="0.14.0" command="cluster playground resize" />

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="MCP">
    Ask your agent which sizes your playground can move to. It calls `list_playground_plans` with the playground named, which answers the plans it can move to with their monthly price. Ask for one, and it calls `resize_playground`, which needs `mcp:write`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    ankra cluster playground resize <cluster-id> --size <plan>
    ```

    A plan the playground cannot move to is refused with the list of those it can.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Check:** once the server is back, `ankra cluster playground status <cluster-id>` shows the new size. A trial is refused with **This playground is a free trial, and a trial keeps its size.**, followed by how to keep it.

## Removing it

A trial playground removes itself after 24 hours. To remove a playground yourself:

<Warning>
Removing a playground deletes everything deployed on it, its storage included. It cannot be undone.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="CLI">
    ```bash
    ankra cluster playground destroy <cluster-id>
    ```

    The command asks first. Follow the teardown with `ankra cluster playground status <cluster-id>` until its phase is `removed`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="MCP">
    Ask your agent to destroy the playground. It calls `destroy_playground`, which needs `mcp:write`.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Playground or your own cluster

| | Playground | Your own cluster |
|---|---|---|
| **Set up** | One press, two to four minutes | One Helm command on your cluster |
| **Who runs it** | Ankra, on Ankra Cloud | You, wherever it runs |
| **What you need** | Nothing for the free trial | A payment method on the organisation, `helm` and `kubectl` access |
| **Size** | 2 vCPU and 4 GB to start, shared with Kubernetes itself | Whatever your cluster has |
| **How long** | 24 hours free, or kept with a card | Until you remove it |

To connect a cluster you already run, see [Connect your own cluster](/get-started/connect-your-cluster).
