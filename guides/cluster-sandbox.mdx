## What Is the Playground?

The playground is a real, writable Kubernetes environment Ankra provisions just for you - a virtual cluster with its own API server, running on Ankra's infrastructure. Unlike a static demo, everything works: you deploy actual stacks through the actual pipeline, browse live resources, stream real logs, and ask the AI to build and ship things - all before connecting a cluster of your own.

<Note>
Playgrounds are provisioned on Ankra's shared playground infrastructure and isolated per organisation. The **free Trial is limited to one per organisation** and expires once everyone has been away for a while — **3 hours on the free plan, a week on a paid one** — with a heads-up notification and email first, and signing back in resets the clock. **Paid sizes can be many** and do not idle-expire: each stays until you destroy it, bounded by your organisation's playground memory budget (below).
</Note>

<Note>
The playground replaces the old sandbox demo and is available to every organisation - there is nothing to enable. Organisations created before the change may still have a "sandbox-demo" cluster listed; it keeps working, but its deploys are simulated rather than run on real infrastructure. Start a playground for the real thing.
</Note>

## Starting a Playground

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the playground">
    On your first visit to Ankra you are offered the choice: connect your own cluster or try the playground. You can also start one any time from <b>Clusters</b> → <b>Create cluster</b>, where <b>Playground</b> is the first Ankra Managed option - it opens a size picker, with the free Trial preselected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait about two minutes">
    Provisioning a real environment takes one to three minutes. The progress screen walks through the phases: reserving, starting the cluster, connecting the agent, deploying the starter stack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the tour">
    You land on the cluster overview with a short tour that opens on your starter stack deploying live, then hands you the controls: deploy something yourself with the AI (press <code>⌘+J</code>).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Try asking the AI: <i>"deploy nginx with 2 replicas"</i> - it builds the stack and ships it through the same pipeline your production deploys would use.
</Tip>

The picker is not the only door. The same lane is served by the CLI (`ankra cluster playground create`), the API, and **Ankra AI itself** - in chat or through the MCP tools you can ask for the available sizes (with live availability and your budget headroom), order a playground, follow its provisioning, resize it, or destroy it. Anything that costs money asks for your confirmation first, naming the size and the monthly price, and every billing check applies exactly as it does in the picker.

## What You Can Do

Everything that works on an imported cluster works here:

- **Deploy stacks** - build them visually or ask the AI, and watch them come alive.
- **Browse live Kubernetes resources** - real pods, deployments, services, and events.
- **Stream logs and open terminals** - it is a real cluster.
- **Use the AI assistant** - analyse, deploy, troubleshoot.
- **Explore the activity feed** - every deploy and event, as it happens.

## Sizes and Pricing

The playground comes in sizes, ordered from the size picker in **Create cluster** → **Playground**. The vCPU and memory a size advertises are yours to deploy into: the platform components that run the environment (the virtual cluster's control plane, CoreDNS, the Ankra agent) are reserved on top of the size, not carved out of it.

| Size      | For your workloads | Storage | Monthly price |
| --------- | ------------------ | ------- | ------------- |
| **Trial** | 1 vCPU / 2 GB      | 20 GB   | Free - expires when the organisation goes idle |
| Small     | 2 vCPU / 4 GB      | 50 GB   | €13.50        |
| Medium    | 4 vCPU / 8 GB      | 100 GB  | €28.80        |
| Large     | 8 vCPU / 16 GB     | 200 GB  | €72.00        |
| Extra large | 16 vCPU / 32 GB  | 400 GB  | €144.00       |

Ordering a paid size requires billing permissions in your organisation, a billing plan, and a payment card on file - the billing pieces are set up on the billing page, and the picker says so before the order if anything is missing. The free Trial needs none of that. The price you order at is the price of record: later catalog changes apply to new orders only, never to an environment you are already running. Billing runs pro-rata from the moment the environment is ready until you destroy it, and every order, resize and destroy leaves a notification receipt. Unpaid invoices eventually pause the lane: new paid orders refuse while an invoice is overdue, and paid playgrounds of an organisation three weeks past due are reclaimed (with warnings well before).

### Resizing

A paid playground resizes in place from **Settings → General → Playground size** (or `ankra cluster playground resize <id> --size <plan>`): only the namespace quota and the monthly price change, billed pro-rata across the change - the old price until the resize, the new price after. Nothing you deployed is touched. The free Trial has one fixed shape; ordering a paid size is the upgrade path.

### Memory budget and limit increases

Paid playgrounds share a per-organisation memory budget: the sum of their advertised memory, **8 GB by default** (the free Trial does not count). The size picker shows how much of the budget your playgrounds use, and a size that does not fit offers a **limit-increase request** right there — the new limit and a short justification, reviewed by the Ankra team, with a notification when it is decided.

The free monthly AI allowance works the same way: the allowance card on the billing page offers **Request a limit increase** when you need more platform-funded AI usage than the plan includes.

A size the shared pool cannot place right now shows as unavailable in the picker rather than disappearing - pick a smaller size or try again shortly.

## Limits

- One free Trial per organisation (recreate it after it expires); paid playgrounds are bounded by the organisation's memory budget.
- A fixed resource budget (quota-enforced) and egress restricted to what deploys need - the playground is for trying Ankra, not for hosting workloads.
- No GitOps or cluster-access (kubeconfig) wiring - those are for clusters you own.

## Playground vs. Your Own Cluster

| Feature                | Playground                              | Imported Cluster                         |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Setup                  | One click, ~2 minutes                   | One helm command on your cluster         |
| Ownership              | Ankra-hosted, ephemeral                 | Yours, permanent                         |
| Write operations       | Fully supported                         | Fully supported                          |
| Resources              | Quota-capped budget                     | Whatever your cluster has                |
| GitOps                 | Not available                           | Optional - add a repo for an audit trail |
| Lifetime               | Trial expires when idle; paid sizes stay | Forever                                 |
