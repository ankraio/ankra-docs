Ankra hosted logs keeps a cluster's container logs for you. The cluster's agent sends the log lines of the cluster's containers to Ankra, where they are kept for up to 7 days and can be searched from **Cluster logs → History** and from each application's **Logs → Search**. There is no logging stack to install, run or connect.

Hosted logs are off by default and turned on per cluster. Nothing leaves a cluster until someone with permission to change it turns them on.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What is sent** | The log lines of the cluster's running containers: the agent follows up to 100 containers at once by default, and none in a namespace listed in its `logs_ship.exclude_namespaces`. [Agent Helm values](/reference/agent-helm-values#hosted-logs) has both limits |
| **Kept for** | Up to 7 days, in Ankra's own log store |
| **Price** | Included until 31 December 2026. From 1 January 2027, 5 GB a month free per organisation, then EUR 0.25 per GB. [Price](#price) has the details |
| **Who can read them** | Members of your organisation whose role holds `kubernetes.logs` on the cluster: every built-in role but `viewer` |
| **Who can turn them on or off** | People with the `clusters.write` permission on the cluster |

- **History starts a minute before you turn it on.** The agent reads each container's log from one minute before that moment (its default). Apart from that minute, lines written while hosted logs are off are never sent.
- **An agent upgrade leaves no gap.** When the cluster's agent is upgraded or restarted, the new one continues each container's log from the last line the old one sent, so no line is stored twice and none is skipped. That holds for a restart of up to 15 minutes, on agent 2.1.1345 or later: the upgrade to that release is the last one that can store up to a minute of lines twice. An agent that is killed, by a lost node or by running out of memory, starts each container a minute back instead: up to a minute of lines can be stored twice, and lines written earlier than that while no agent ran are not sent.
- **A search reads one cluster.** Every read of the hosted log store is limited to your organisation and the cluster you are reading, whatever the query says, so no other cluster's lines can come back. The note under the lines in an application's **Logs → Search**, and the `cluster_attribution` note of an API or MCP answer, says so.
- **Your own log store comes first.** While a [log source](/integrations/log-sources) of your own is connected to the cluster, History on **Automatic** searches that store. Choose **Ankra hosted logs** under **Log store** in Cluster logs to search the hosted lines instead.
- **Live logs need none of this.** **Live** reads the running containers through the cluster's agent, with or without hosted logs.

<Note>
Ankra turns hosted logs on platform by platform. Where they are not offered, you can still turn them on for a cluster, and nothing is sent until they are. The cluster's **Logs and data** settings and `ankra cluster logs-ship status` say which applies to yours.
</Note>

## Price

Hosted logs are included until 31 December 2026. From 1 January 2027:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Free each month** | 5 GB per organisation, across all of its clusters |
| **Above that** | EUR 0.25 per GB |
| **What a GB is** | 1,000,000,000 bytes of log text as the containers wrote it, after an overlong line is cut at 16 KiB. What the log store adds to each line, such as its cluster, namespace, pod and container, is not counted, and neither is the compressed size |
| **Kept for** | Up to 7 days, at no extra cost |
| **Month** | The calendar month, in UTC |

- **At the free 5 GB, sending pauses.** When an organisation's clusters have sent 5 GB in a month and paid logs are not on, its clusters stop sending until the first day of the next month. Lines already sent stay searchable for their 7 days, **Live** keeps working, and lines written while sending is paused stay in the nodes' own container log files.
- **Paid logs have a monthly limit.** An owner turns on paid logs and sets a limit in euros for the month. Sending then goes on past the free 5 GB until the month's charge reaches that limit, and pauses the same way. Nothing is charged for hosted logs unless an owner has turned paid logs on. [Paid logs](#paid-logs) shows how.
- **Owners are told** when the month's logs reach 80% and 100% of the free 5 GB, and of the limit when paid logs are on.
- **See it before it applies.** The bytes are recorded today as `hosted_logs_ingested_bytes` on the [usage](/platform/usage) report, so you can see what a month of your clusters' logs comes to. The clusters that used hosted logs in November 2026 sent 2 to 3 GB of log text a month each.
- **Ankra built-in metrics are not charged.** They stay included in every plan: see [Cluster metrics](/platform/cluster-metrics#ankra-built-in-metrics).

## Paid logs

Paid logs decide what happens past the free 5 GB. They are off until an owner turns them on, so hosted logs cost nothing without that decision. You can turn them on before 1 January 2027: nothing is charged until then, and the limit applies from that month.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Hosted logs in Plan and billing">
    Open **Settings → Plan and billing → Hosted logs** (`/next/organisation/billing?view=hosted-logs`). **This month** shows the log text your clusters have sent since the first of the month against the free 5 GB, and whether they are sending. **Price and paid logs** lists the free amount, the price above it, whether paid logs are on, what this month is charged, how long lines are kept and when owners are told. The **Hosted logs** panel on the **Overview** shows the same month in short.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on paid logs">
    Click **Turn on paid logs** and enter a **Monthly limit, in euros**. Before anything is saved, the dialog says what the limit means, for example `Up to €10 a month. You pay €0.25 per GB above the free 5 GB.` and `€10 covers 40 GB of log text above the free 5 GB each month.` Before 1 January 2027 it also says `Nothing is charged until 1 January 2027. The limit applies from then.` Log text sent earlier in a month above the free 5 GB counts toward that month's charge once paid logs are on, so turning them on part way through a month counts what was already sent, never above the limit. Click **Turn on paid logs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the month">
    The view reads `Sending. Paid logs are on, up to €10 a month.`, and from 1 January 2027 a second meter, **Charged against your monthly limit**, shows what the month comes to. When the month's logs were paused at the free 5 GB, your clusters start sending again within a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- **Change the limit at any time.** Click **Change the limit**, enter the new amount and click **Save the limit**. The limit is more than €0 and at most €1,000,000. Raising the limit of a month that is paused at it starts sending again. A limit at or below what the month already comes to pauses sending as soon as you save it, and what was already sent is still charged.
- **Turn paid logs off** with **Turn off paid logs**. From 1 January 2027, sending then pauses once the month's log text reaches the free 5 GB, or right away when it is already past it, until the first day of the next month. What the month already comes to is still charged, and your limit is kept for the next time you turn paid logs on.
- **Who can change it.** People whose role holds `billing.manage`, which owners have. Anyone with `billing.read` sees the view without the buttons, and the page says who can change it.
- **What paid logs need first.** A paid plan with billing set up, and a payment method or prepaid credit. Until then the view says what to do first, such as `Paid hosted logs need a paid plan with billing set up. Choose a plan on the Plan and billing page first.`, and has no **Turn on paid logs** button. When Ankra cannot check your billing account at that moment, the view and the dialog say `Ankra could not check your billing account just now. Try again in a minute.` and nothing is turned on.
- **Over MCP**, `get_hosted_logs_billing` reads the setting and the month so far, and `set_hosted_logs_billing` turns paid logs on with a limit, changes the limit or turns them off. See [MCP Tools](/platform/mcp-tools).

### When sending pauses

A pause applies to every cluster of the organisation, whatever each cluster's own switch says.

| What Plan and billing says | Why | What to do |
|----------------------------|-----|------------|
| `Paused at the free 5 GB.` | This month's log text reached the free 5 GB and paid logs are off | Turn on paid logs |
| `Paused at your monthly limit of €10.` | Paid logs are on and the month's charge reached the limit | Raise the limit |
| `Paused: there is no credit left to pay for log text above the free 5 GB.` | A prepaid organisation's [credit](/platform/billing#credit) ran out | Add credit in the **Credit** view |

While a month is paused, each cluster's **Ankra hosted logs** section reads `On, but paused.` with the reason and when it ends, for example `Your organisation has used its free 5 GB of log text this month, and paid logs are off. Nothing is sent until 1 February 2027.` Someone who can manage billing gets a link to the one change: **Turn on paid logs**, **Raise the monthly limit** or **Add credit**. Anyone else reads whom to ask, such as `Someone who can manage billing in your organisation can turn on paid logs.` When a search of an application's **Logs** or a cluster's **Cluster logs** finds nothing, it reads **Ankra hosted logs are paused for your organisation this month.** with the same reason and link. Lines sent before the pause stay searchable for their 7 days, and **Live** keeps working.

### Notices

Owners are told before and when sending pauses, by email to the organisation's billing address and in the notification bell:

| When | The notice |
|------|------------|
| This month's log text reaches 80% of the free 5 GB, and paid logs are off | `Your organisation has used 80% of its free hosted logs this month` |
| It reaches the free 5 GB, and paid logs are off | `Hosted logs are paused for the rest of the month` |
| The month's charge reaches 80% of the monthly limit | `Hosted logs reached 80% of your monthly limit` |
| It reaches the monthly limit | `Hosted logs reached your monthly limit and are paused` |

Each notice says what was used, what happens next and the one change that keeps your clusters sending, with a link to Plan and billing.

## Before you start

- **The cluster's agent must follow the switch.** An agent that is too old, or that has log shipping turned off in its own Helm values (`logs_ship.enabled`), sends nothing whatever the switch says. Upgrade it with `ankra cluster agent upgrade --cluster <cluster>`, or turn on automatic agent upgrades in **Settings → Agent**. See [Cluster Settings](/platform/cluster-settings).
- **Your role needs `clusters.write` on the cluster.** Anyone else is told which roles can turn hosted logs on. See [Roles & Access](/guides/roles-and-access).

## Turn it on for a cluster

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Portal">
1. Open **Infrastructure → Clusters → &lt;your cluster&gt; → Settings → Logs and data** (`/next/clusters/<cluster-id>/settings/logs`). The **Ankra hosted logs** section reads `Off. No log lines from this cluster are sent to Ankra.`, with what is sent, how long it is kept, the price, who can read the lines and who can change the setting.
2. Click **Turn on hosted logs**. The **Turn on Ankra hosted logs** dialog repeats what is sent and for how long, and that lines written while it is off are never sent later.
3. Click **Turn on hosted logs** in the dialog. The section now reads `On. This cluster's agent sends the log lines of its containers to Ankra.`

To manage several clusters at once, open **Managed services → Hosted telemetry** (`/next/services/telemetry`). **Collection by cluster** lists each cluster with **Collection requested** or **Collection off** and a **Turn on hosted logs** or **Turn off hosted logs** button. Turning collection on requests log shipping; it does not verify that lines have arrived.
</Tab>
<Tab title="CLI">
<CliVersion since="0.24.0" />

Check the cluster first:

```bash
ankra cluster logs-ship status --cluster <cluster>
```

The status says whether the cluster ships its logs, whether hosted logs are offered on your platform, and whether the cluster's agent follows the switch. Then turn it on:

```bash
ankra cluster logs-ship enable --cluster <cluster>
```

`enable` says what is sent and asks before it turns anything on. `--yes` answers the question for scripts.
</Tab>
<Tab title="MCP">
`get_cluster_hosted_log_shipping` reads whether a cluster ships its container logs to Ankra and whether its agent supports the switch. `set_cluster_hosted_log_shipping` turns shipping on or off. See [MCP Tools](/platform/mcp-tools).
</Tab>
</Tabs>

Hosted logs are turned on for the whole cluster, so every application with an environment on that cluster gets log history from then on.

## Turn it on from an application

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the application's Logs">
    Open **Applications → &lt;your application&gt; → Logs**. When the cluster keeps no logs, the page reads **Live** with a card above the lines. The card shows **&lt;namespace&gt; has no log history yet** with `Ankra will keep the container logs from <cluster> for up to 7 days. Included until 31 December 2026, then 5 GB a month free per organisation and €0.25 per GB.` and `This covers every namespace on <cluster>, not only <namespace>.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on log history">
    Click **Turn on log history**. The card reads **Turning on log history** and ticks off **Turned on Ankra hosted logs**, then **Waiting for the first line from &lt;namespace&gt;**.

    If your role cannot change the cluster, the card names the role that can, and **Ask an admin to turn it on** copies a link to this view: whoever opens it lands here and can turn it on with one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the first line">
    When **First line from &lt;namespace&gt; arrived** is ticked, the card reads **Log history is on** and the lines show in **Search**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To keep the logs in your own store instead, click **Use your own log store instead**. See [Log Sources](/integrations/log-sources).

## Verify

Open **Infrastructure → Clusters → &lt;your cluster&gt; → Technical details → Kubernetes → Cluster logs**, click **History**, and set **Log store** in **Filters** to **Ankra hosted logs**. Lines written since you turned hosted logs on appear. The first lines usually arrive within a minute; until then the page reads **Waiting for the first lines** and checks every few seconds. See [Logs](/platform/logs).

## What the Logs page tells you

When an application's **Logs** page has nothing to show, it says why, with the next step:

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| **&lt;namespace&gt; has no log history yet**, with **Turn on log history** | Hosted logs are offered here but off for this cluster | Click **Turn on log history**, or **Ask an admin to turn it on** |
| `Ankra hosted logs are on for <cluster>, but no line from <namespace> has arrived in the last hour.` | Hosted logs are on, but nothing from the application's namespace arrived | Click **Let Ankra look into it**: Ankra AI reads the cluster's logging setup and changes nothing while it looks. Or read **Live** logs. |
| `No log store is connected to <cluster>, and Ankra hosted logs are not offered here yet.` | Neither hosted logs nor a store of your own keeps this cluster's logs | Click **Use your own log store instead** |
| **This cluster's agent cannot send logs to Ankra yet.** | The agent is too old to follow the switch, or log shipping is off in its own settings | Upgrade the agent from the card, or open **Agent version and automatic upgrades** |
| **Waiting for the first logs from &lt;cluster&gt;.** | Hosted logs were turned on a moment ago | Wait a minute or two. Nothing written more than a minute before it was turned on is sent. |
| **Ankra's hosted logs hold nothing from this cluster in the &lt;time range&gt;.** | Whether hosted logs are on could not be checked just now | Click **Check again** |
| **Ankra hosted logs are paused for your organisation this month.** | From 1 January 2027: the month reached the free 5 GB with paid logs off, reached the paid logs limit, or ran out of prepaid credit | Follow the link to Plan and billing, or ask someone who can manage billing. See [When sending pauses](#when-sending-pauses) |
| **Logs from &lt;namespace&gt; are arriving** | Lines arrived in the last hour, but not in this time range or with these filters | Widen the time range or clear the filters |

## Turn it off

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Portal">
In **Infrastructure → Clusters → &lt;your cluster&gt; → Settings → Logs and data**, click **Turn off hosted logs**, read the **Turn off Ankra hosted logs** dialog and confirm with **Turn off hosted logs**. The section reads `Off. No log lines from this cluster are sent to Ankra.`
</Tab>
<Tab title="CLI">
<CliVersion since="0.24.0" />

```bash
ankra cluster logs-ship disable --cluster <cluster>
```
</Tab>
<Tab title="MCP">
Call `set_cluster_hosted_log_shipping` to turn shipping off.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

The cluster stops sending its container logs to Ankra within a minute. Lines already sent are kept for up to 7 days. Lines written while hosted logs are off are not sent, except the last minute before someone turns them on again.
