The AI Gateway setup connects Ankra AI to your code host and your team's chat, and routes the notifications that matter to the right place. When you finish, Ankra AI reviews pull requests in the repositories you chose, answers questions in Slack or Teams, and posts cluster notifications to a channel.

The setup is a guide, not a settings page of its own: each step reads what is already connected, links to the settings page that does the work, and checks again when you come back. Moving between steps does not switch anything on. How the gateway carries a message or a pull request event to Ankra AI and back is explained in [AI Gateway](/pipelines/ai-gateway).

## Before you start

- **Pull requests, Slack and Teams** need a role that manages Ankra AI, such as owner or admin. Without it, the step reads **Needs an administrator** and says **Ask an organisation administrator to configure this. You can still manage your personal preferences.**
- **Cluster notifications** need a role that manages alerts.
- **Email & personal updates** is your own profile: anyone can do it.
- For pull requests, a GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket account that can install an app or add a connection for your repositories. See [GitHub](/integrations/github), [GitLab](/integrations/gitlab) or [Bitbucket Cloud](/integrations/bitbucket-cloud).
- For Slack, permission to add an app to your Slack workspace. For Teams, permission to install apps from the Microsoft Teams Store; a Teams administrator is needed only if your tenant restricts Store apps.

## Open the setup

Go to **Ankra AI → Gateway** (`/next/ai/gateway`). The page lists **Where your team works with Ankra**: **Slack**, **Microsoft Teams**, **Pull requests**, **Watch and alert delivery**, and **Email and your own updates**, each with a badge such as **Connected** or **Partly connected**.

Press **Set up the gateway**. While steps are left, the button shows how many are done, for example **Set up the gateway (2 of 5)**; once all are, it reads **Review the setup**. The **Set up AI Gateway** page opens (`/next/ai/setup`) with **Setup progress** and the five steps. Each step shows its state: **Set up**, **Partly set up**, **Not set up**, **Could not check** or **Needs an administrator**.

Every step has the same shape: **What Ankra found** lists what is already connected, **How to set it up** lists what to do, and the links open the settings page that does it. Save on that page, then come back: the step checks again.

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pull requests">
    **Set up AI reviews for pull requests.**

    1. Connect GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket. **Configure GitHub reviews**, **Configure GitLab reviews** and **Configure Bitbucket reviews** open that connection in **Gateway connections**.
    2. Enable AI review for the repositories you choose. Enable mention replies if you want to ask questions in pull request comments with `@ankraai`.
    3. Start in **Ask** mode. Automatic reviews are read-only; **Agent** mode also lets Ankra AI open a separate fix pull request where supported, which a person merges.

    See [AI code review](/platform/ai-code-review).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Slack">
    **Connect Ankra AI to Slack.**

    1. **Configure Slack workspace** opens **Gateway connections**. Press **Add to Slack** and approve Ankra in Slack's installation screen.
    2. Choose **Ask** or **Agent** mode for the workspace in **Gateway connections**. Start in **Ask** to investigate and explain.
    3. Link your personal Slack account under **Your account → Connected accounts**, and choose your own answer-ready messages under **Your account → Notifications**.
    4. To send alerts to a Slack channel, use **Send alerts to a channel** on this step: pick the **Channel** and the **Notifications to send**, then press **Send these to** and the channel. Ankra adds the channel as a notification destination and a routing rule for these notifications, for every cluster.

    The card confirms **Notifications are on their way to the channel**. See [AI connections](/platform/ai-connections).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teams">
    **Connect Ankra AI to Microsoft Teams.**

    1. Install Ankra AI from the Microsoft Teams Store, then follow the connection steps on this page and enter the claim code Ankra AI posts.
    2. Choose **Ask** or **Agent** mode for the tenant in **Gateway connections**. Start in **Ask** to investigate and explain.
    3. Link your personal Teams account under **Your account → Connected accounts**, and choose your own answer-ready messages under **Your account → Notifications**.
    4. To send alerts to a Teams channel, use **Send alerts to a channel** on this step, as for Slack.

    **Configure Microsoft Teams** opens the tenant's connection. See [AI connections](/platform/ai-connections).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email & personal updates">
    **Choose the notifications you receive.** This step changes your own account only.

    1. **Review my contact email** opens **Your account → Profile**: check that messages reach the right address.
    2. **Open my notification preferences** opens **Your account → Notifications**: choose email subscriptions, delivery channels and the minimum severity, and whether linked Slack and Teams accounts tell you when an AI answer is ready.

    Shared channel destinations and cluster routing rules are managed separately by your organisation, in the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cluster notifications">
    **Choose where cluster notifications go.**

    1. With Slack or Teams connected, pick a channel in **Send alerts to a channel** to send the recommended notifications there in one action.
    2. For anything else, **Set notification destinations** opens **Integrations & alerts → Notification destinations**: add a destination, such as a webhook, and send a test delivery.
    3. **Configure cluster routing rules** opens **Integrations & alerts → Notification routes**: create a routing rule with event kinds, severity and a cluster filter, or leave the cluster filter empty to cover all clusters.
    4. Preview which destinations match, then send a test through the rule and check that it arrives.

    The step lists **Cluster events to consider**: **Availability** (an offline agent or degraded resource health), **Changes** (failed operations, failed deployments or GitOps sync failures), **Alerts** (a configured alert rule fires, with AI analysis where available) and **Security** (newly detected severe vulnerabilities). Pipeline run notifications are organisation events, so a cluster filter does not match them. See [Notification routing](/guides/notification-routing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Next step** and **Previous step** move between steps; **Finish guide** on the last step returns you to Home.

## Verify

1. Open **Ankra AI → Gateway** again. Each surface you connected shows **Connected**, and the button reads **Review the setup** once all five steps are done.
2. On the **Set up AI Gateway** page, **Setup progress** reads **Everything here is set up.**
3. Open a pull request in a repository you enabled. Ankra AI posts its review on it, and the review is listed under **Ankra AI → Code reviews**.
4. In a Slack channel the app is in, ask Ankra a question. It answers in the thread.

## What can go wrong

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs an administrator** on a step | Your role cannot manage Ankra AI, or alerts for the last step | Ask an organisation owner or admin to do that step. The personal step stays open to you |
| **Your role can view these settings. Ask an administrator to make changes.** | Your role can read the settings page but not change it | As above |
| **Could not check** on a step | Ankra could not read whether it is connected | Reload the page. **Your access could not be checked. Reload to try again.** means the same |
| **The channels could not be loaded** in **Send alerts to a channel** | Ankra could not list the channels it can post to | Check that the Slack app or the Teams app is still installed, then reload |
| **The notifications were not set up** | Adding the destination or the routing rule failed | Read the message under it, then add them by hand under **Integrations & alerts** |

For Ankra AI not answering at all, see [Troubleshooting Ankra AI](/guides/troubleshooting-ankra-ai).
