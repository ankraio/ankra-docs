Ankra tells you about what happens in your organisation in two places. **Notifications**, opened from the bell, is everything Ankra reported: failed operations, firing alerts, agent and security notices, and the messages addressed to you. **Needs you**, in the sidebar, is the shorter list of what waits on a person: an approval, a fix, an alert, each with the one thing to do about it.

Neither page decides who else hears about a notification. That is done by [notification destinations and routes](/guides/notification-routing), by each [alert rule](/guides/alerts)'s own destinations, and by your own channels under **Your account → Notifications** ([phone and tablet](/guides/mobile-push)).

## Notifications

The bell opens **Notifications** (`/next/notifications`): Platform and Cloud activity, with one place to review what needs attention. It has three views:

| View | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| **Inbox** | Current notifications across Platform and Cloud. Reading a notification only changes your unread count. |
| **Needs you** | Open warnings and critical notifications. Decisions Ankra AI is waiting on are under **Open AI Work**. |
| **History** | Resolved Platform notifications and notices you archived. Archiving does not resolve the underlying issue. |

Above the list you can search (**Title, message or cluster**), narrow by **Product** (**Platform and Cloud**, **Platform**, **Cloud**) and by **Audience** (**Everyone**, **Addressed to me**, **Organisation**), and switch on **Unread only**. **Mark shown as read** marks every notification on the page as read. Each notification's menu has **Mark as read** (or **Mark as unread**) and **Archive for me** (or **Restore to inbox**).

**Summarise this page** asks Ankra AI to summarise the notifications shown and say what needs attention first. Opening one notification shows its details in a side panel, with **Investigate with AI** under **Ask Ankra**. Both need access to Ankra AI; without it the panel says **AI access is required to start an investigation.**

**Notification preferences** at the top of the page opens **Your account → Notifications**, where you choose how notifications addressed to you reach you.

### For you, and for the organisation

A notification is addressed either to the whole organisation, such as a failed deployment or a firing alert, or to you personally, such as a failed pipeline run you started or pushed. **Audience → Addressed to me** shows only yours.

Reading and archiving are yours alone. Archiving shows **Archived for you. The underlying issue is unchanged.**, and the notification stays open for everyone else.

## One notification

A notification's own page (`/next/notifications/<id>`) says what happened and what to do:

- **What happened** - the notification's message.
- **AI analysis** - when Ankra has analysed it, a summary, **Recommended actions** and **Read full analysis**. While the analysis is being made the section reads **Analysis is being prepared. This page updates automatically.**
- **How to fix it** - the steps, a button to the page they name, and **Ask Ankra to investigate**. For a notification that cleared on its own the steps fold under **If it happens again, here is how to fix it**.
- **Acknowledge** - records that you have seen an open notification.
- **Mute on this cluster** - for a notification about a cluster. It reads **Muted for you on** the cluster's name, and new notifications like it from that cluster stop showing for you, including in the critical issues email. Everyone else still gets them. **Manage what you muted** opens **Integrations & alerts → Muted notifications**, where **Unmute** ends it.

When several critical issues are open, **Previous issue** and **Next issue** step through them.

## How notifications clear

A failed operation and the notification about it are two different records. The operation stays in the cluster's history as failed for good (see [Operations](/platform/operations#failed-history-versus-open-problems)). The notification is the part that clears:

- **On its own.** Ankra re-checks open notifications about once a minute. A failed-operation notification resolves once a later run of the same operation on that cluster succeeds, one about a platform resource resolves when that resource's latest step succeeds or the resource is gone, and a GitOps failure resolves once the cluster reports itself in sync again. Resolved notifications move to **History**.
- **By hand.** **Acknowledge** records that you saw it. **Archive for me** takes it out of your **Inbox** and into your **History**, for you only.

So a cluster that has recovered has few or no open notifications, while its operations history still lists every failed attempt.

## Needs you

**Needs you** (`/next/needs-you`) collects every approval, fix and alert that waits on a person, once, with the one thing to do about it. Its title counts what is waiting. When nothing is, it reads **Nothing waits on you.**

It groups what it finds into sections. You see a section only when your role can see what it lists, and the cost sections only when the organisation has the Cost area:

| Section | What waits |
|---------|-----------|
| **Applications** | Your applications that need attention |
| **Managed services** | Your managed services that wait on you |
| **Security** | Security fixes that wait for a yes, and fixes you started that went wrong |
| **Security fixes Ankra opened** | Pull requests Ankra opened to fix a vulnerability in an application |
| **Cost** | Cost decisions waiting for you |
| **Cost automation** | Cost automation changes |
| **Budgets** | Budgets that need you |
| **Held while paused** | What Ankra held back for paused applications, with the option to resume where your role allows it |
| **Ask Ankra** | Questions Ankra AI asked you in your conversations |
| **Alerts** | Alerts that wait on a person |

An item marked **Urgent** comes first. A section that could not be read says so and offers **Try again**, so an unread section never passes for one with nothing waiting.

## What the portal does not have

Notifications cannot be snoozed in the portal. To hold back an alert firing for a while, use `ankra alerts triggers snooze`, see [Alerts](/guides/alerts). There is no list of recent Stack drafts and no dashboard layout to arrange: **Home** opens with a question to Ankra AI, and under it what needs you.
