An organisation's settings are spread over the areas of the sidebar, each next to the work it governs. **Settings** itself holds two pages: **Organisation** (name, logo, short name, leave or delete) and **Plan and billing**. This page lists where every other setting lives, then describes the Organisation page and the few organisation-wide settings that used to sit beside it.

## Where each setting lives

The sidebar's **Settings and help** group holds **Identity & access**, **Settings** and **Support**. **Infrastructure**, **Build & delivery**, **Integrations & alerts** and **Ankra AI** sit in the **Platform** group above it. In Infrastructure, Build & delivery and Integrations & alerts the settings pages are listed under **Configuration** in the area's menu.

## The Organisation page

Open **Settings → Organisation** (`/next/organisation`). The page has two sections, **About** and **Leave or delete**. Owners and admins can change it. Anyone else sees the same page read-only, with a line that names their role and the people who can change it.

### Rename the organisation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the name">
    In **About**, type the new name in **Name** (up to 100 characters). **Save name** and **Undo** appear once the name differs from the saved one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Click **Save name**. The page reads **Saved. Everyone sees `<new name>` from now on.**, and the organisation switcher shows the new name.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The name is what everyone in the organisation sees in the switcher, on invitations and on invoices.

### Change the logo

Click **Change logo…**, then drop an image on **Drop an image here**: PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 512 KiB and 1024 by 1024 pixels. It is uploaded the moment you choose it, and the dialog reads **Saved. Everyone in `<organisation>` sees the new logo.** **Remove logo** puts the organisation's initials back. The logo shows in the organisation switcher and on these settings, for everyone in the organisation.

### Short name and organisation ID

**Short name** is the organisation's fixed identifier for scripts. It never changes when you rename the organisation, so commands that use it keep working. The page shows the command that switches the CLI to this organisation:

<CliVersion since="0.13.0" />

```bash
ankra org switch <short-name>
```

`ankra org switch` also takes the organisation's name or ID, and `ankra org current` shows which organisation the CLI is using.

The **Organisation ID** is under **Technical details**, which shows once you turn on **Show technical details** in the account menu.

### Leave or delete

**Leave `<organisation>`** takes only you out: you lose access at once and your tokens for the organisation stop working. The only owner cannot leave until they make someone else an owner. **Delete `<organisation>`** is for owners only (admins see an **Owners only** badge) and is refused while the organisation still has clusters, other members or unpaid invoices. The steps, the refusals and what deleting removes are in [Leave or delete an organisation](/guides/delete-or-leave-an-organisation).

## Cluster agent upgrades

Whether Ankra upgrades every cluster's agent on its own is set in **Build & delivery → Builds and clusters**, section **Cluster agents**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Go to **Build & delivery → Builds and clusters** (`/next/organisation/pipelines`) and scroll to **Cluster agents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the switch on or off">
    Turn **Upgrade cluster agents automatically** on or off. On, the page reads **Saved. Each cluster's agent upgrades on its own when a new version is out.** Off, it reads **Saved. Each cluster shows when a new agent is out, and you upgrade it from there.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the switch is on, Ankra upgrades the agent on every cluster a few at a time, unless a cluster's own settings say not to: see [Cluster settings](/platform/cluster-settings). Upgrading the agent does not change your apps. Without permission to change the setting you see its state as **On** or **Off**.

## DNS records

**Infrastructure → Domains** (`/next/organisation/domains`) holds the names your apps answer on. Two of its sections are organisation-wide settings.

### Records on your Ankra domain

The section **`<organisation>`'s Ankra domain** shows the domain Ankra made for the organisation and the records you added under it. Ankra makes the domain when the organisation's first cluster gets a name; until then the section reads **No Ankra domain yet**. Owners and admins see **Add record** once the domain is **Active**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a record">
    Click **Add record**. Fill in **Name** (the label, for example `logs`; the domain is added after it), **Type** (CNAME, A or TXT), **Value** and **Time to live** (**Auto**, **1 minute**, **5 minutes**, **30 minutes** or **1 hour**), then click **Add record**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to go live">
    The record shows **Being set up**, then **Active** once it answers in DNS. A record Ankra could not write shows **Failed** with **Ankra could not write it. Change it, or delete it and add it again.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or delete a record">
    Open the record's options menu. **Change…** edits its value and time to live. **Delete…** asks **Delete `<name>`?**; **Delete record** stops the name answering within a few minutes. To change a record's name or type, delete it and add it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The CLI manages the same records, and also takes AAAA records:

```bash
ankra org dns zone                                        # the organisation's Ankra domain
ankra org dns list                                        # its records
ankra org dns add logs CNAME lb-1234.example-lb.com --ttl 300
ankra org dns update logs target.example.com
ankra org dns delete logs
```

### Cluster domains

The section **Cluster names** on the same page has one switch per cluster. On, the cluster gets its own name under the organisation's Ankra domain, and its apps answer on addresses under it. Turning a switch off asks **Take `<cluster>`'s name away?**: apps on that name stop answering within a few minutes, and your own domains on the cluster keep working. Confirm with **Take the name away**. A cluster whose name could not be set up offers **Try again**.

```bash
ankra org dns zones                     # every cluster's name and its state
ankra cluster domain <cluster>          # read one cluster's name (creates nothing)
ankra cluster domain <cluster> --enable
ankra cluster domain <cluster> --remove
```

To serve apps on a domain you own, from **Add your own domain** on the same page, follow [Use your own domain](/guides/use-your-own-domain). For a domain held in Avura, see [Use Avura domains with Ankra](/guides/avura-domains).

## Projects

When projects are turned on for your organisation, **Identity & access → Projects** lists them, the Default project first. Members whose role holds `projects.manage` (owners and admins) create, rename and delete projects there; everyone else sees the list read-only. A project that still holds resources cannot be deleted: the page names what it holds, and you move those to another project or make them available to the whole organisation first. To limit someone to one project, give them the **Project only** role on **Identity & access → People**: see [Project-only members](/concepts/projects#project-only-members) and [Set up projects](/guides/set-up-projects).

## Ankra AI settings

Ankra AI's settings are in the **Ankra AI** area, under **Configuration**: **Models and provider**, **Skills and tools**, **Autonomy and approvals**, **AI workspaces**, **Memory**, **Feedback** and **Gateway connections**. See [Ankra AI](/platform/ai-overview) for what each one controls.
