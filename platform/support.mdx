Get help with Ankra Cloud and Platform through the shared Support center. Start an AI chat, contact the team directly, or follow an existing conversation. Native linked-ticket reviews and CLI support workflows remain available for existing Platform requests.

## Shared Cloud and Platform support

Open [Ankra Support](https://support.ankra.app) for an AI chat or choose **Contact Ankra** to send a message directly to the team. Cloud and Platform both use this support center.

Support reuses its existing session. If it needs a new session, it automatically continues through the product you last used for Support; a direct first visit uses Cloud. If that product needs you to sign in, complete its normal sign-in and return to your chat. You can choose **Continue with Platform** or **Continue with Cloud** if the automatic connection does not finish. Signing out of Support keeps you signed out in that browser tab until you choose to continue again. Documentation remains available without signing in.

An unsent AI question is restored for review after reconnecting within ten minutes, in the same browser tab. It is not sent automatically. To bring in the team, choose **Contact Ankra**, review your message and any AI conversation you choose to share, and send it. Follow replies under **Conversations**. A successful submission shows a neutral confirmation with a green accent; red notices indicate an error.

The conversation header shows its status alongside the ticket number. Choose **Close conversation** when you no longer need help, or **Reopen conversation** to continue a closed or resolved case. **Check for replies** refreshes the thread without losing your unsent reply. The original request and team replies appear together above the block editor.

Staff can open [Support administration](https://support.ankra.app/admin), which starts the staff sign-in handoff. Verified `@ankra.io` and `@ankra.ai` accounts can also enter through a Cloud or Platform issuer configured for Ankra staff. Open a case in the Support queue to reply publicly or choose a private staff note. Expand **Manage ticket** to change status, priority or assignment; saving these settings preserves your unsent reply and its visibility. Support access does not grant permission to act on customer infrastructure; Cloud and Platform access grants still apply.

### Staff AI assistance

Open a ticket in the Support queue to use the investigation assistant beside the conversation.

- **Rewrite a reply** - write your draft and choose **AI rewrite**. Review the preview, then choose **Use rewrite** or **Keep original**. You can undo an applied rewrite before editing it further. Rewriting uses only your draft, and never sends a reply automatically.
- **Investigate a case** - ask a question or choose **Summarize case** or **Plan investigation**. The assistant reads the selected ticket, recent messages and official Cloud and Platform docs. It identifies unavailable documentation and omitted older messages.
- **Include private staff notes** - under **Context & connections**, select this only when you want internal staff discussion included. Changing the setting clears the current AI chat.
- **Keep a finding** - choose **Add to private note**, review the draft, then send it when ready. The assistant does not post directly to the customer.

The sidebar chat is kept in the current page only. Clear it with **Clear chat**; switching tickets or signing out also clears it. Live Cloud and Platform admin diagnostics are not connected. Use the native admin links for live checks under your product staff permissions. Treat AI suggestions as findings to verify.

The native linked-ticket review flow and CLI support commands below remain available for existing Platform workflows.

## Where to open a ticket

- **Portal** - in the new Ankra portal, open the **Account** menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Support** to enter the shared support center, then **Contact Ankra** to start a request. In the Classic portal, **Support** in the profile menu opens the built-in Support panel, where **New request** starts a ticket.
- **Command palette** - press `⌘ K` / `Ctrl K` and choose **Support**: in the new Ankra portal it enters the shared support center, in the Classic portal it opens the built-in Support panel. See [Command Palette](/platform/command-palette).
- **CLI** - `ankra support create`, below.
- **AI Assistant** - ask it to raise a ticket, below.

## Linked Platform ticket review

Requests linked to existing Platform operating tickets retain this native review flow. New requests in the shared Support center use **Message → Review and send**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Give the ticket a subject and description, pick a category, and optionally link the cluster it relates to. Context about the selected cluster is attached automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI review">
    Before the ticket is filed, Ankra runs an AI review that enriches it - suggesting a summary, severity, and category - checks quality, may ask clarifying questions, and surfaces likely duplicates of existing tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Accept the review (or answer its clarifying questions) and submit. If a strong duplicate is found, you can link to the existing ticket instead of opening a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and reply">
    Follow status changes, read replies from the Ankra team, and add your own comments and attachments as the conversation continues.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Categories

| Category | Use for |
|----------|---------|
| `technical` | How-to questions and technical problems |
| `bug` | Something is broken or behaving incorrectly |
| `feature_request` | A capability you'd like to see |
| `account` | Access, organisation, and user issues |
| `billing` | Invoices, plans, and payment |
| `other` | Anything that doesn't fit above |

Severity (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`) is suggested by the AI review and can be adjusted. These are the severities the [SLA](https://ankra.ai/sla) sets response times for; `medium` is the SLA's Normal.

## Ticket lifecycle

A ticket moves through these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `pending_review` | Created and undergoing AI review |
| `open` | Filed and awaiting triage |
| `triaging` | Being assessed by the Ankra team |
| `in_progress` | Actively being worked on |
| `waiting_customer` | Waiting on a reply from you |
| `resolved` | A resolution has been provided |
| `closed` | Closed |

Each ticket records its `source` (`portal`, `api`, `cli`, or `agent`), an AI summary, the AI review status, comments (from you, the Ankra team, AI, or the system), and any attachments.

While working a ticket, Ankra support may open a time-limited, read-only support session in your organisation to see what you see, or an elevated one with your consent. See [Ankra Support Access](/security/support-access).

## Attachments

Attach logs, manifests, or screenshots to give the team what they need. Attachments are validated by type and size, and there is a per-ticket limit. Download an attachment again from the ticket at any time.

## From Ankra AI and the MCP server

The assistant and the [MCP server](/platform/mcp-server) work the same tickets. Ask Ankra AI to "raise a support ticket about the ingress 502s" and it proposes a **Raise support ticket** action naming the subject, category, severity and cluster; confirm, and the ticket is filed with source `agent`, tied to the cluster you were chatting about. It can also list your tickets, read one by its number, reply on your behalf, and close one you consider resolved. The tools are `list_support_tickets`, `get_support_ticket`, `create_support_ticket`, `comment_support_ticket` and `close_support_ticket` - see the [MCP Tool Reference](/platform/mcp-tools#support-tickets). Attachments stay in the portal and the CLI.

## From the CLI

<CliVersion since="0.3.0" />

```bash
# Raise a ticket about a cluster
ankra support create \
  --subject "Ingress returns 502 after upgrade" \
  --description "Since the add-on upgrade this morning every request through the ingress returns 502." \
  --category technical \
  --cluster my-cluster

# Follow it
ankra support list
ankra support get <ticket-id>
ankra support comment <ticket-id> -m "Logs attached."
ankra support attach <ticket-id> ./screenshot.png
ankra support close <ticket-id>
```

`create` runs the same AI review as the portal and asks you to confirm when it flags the ticket or finds a likely duplicate; `--force` skips that prompt. See [`ankra support`](/reference/cli/support) for every flag.
