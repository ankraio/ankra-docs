Home is the first page you see when you sign in and the first item in the main menu (`/next`). It opens on Ankra AI: a box to ask a question, three questions to start from, the things that wait on you, and your most recent conversations.

## Before you start

- **Permission to use Ankra AI.** Home is built on Ankra AI, which needs the `ai.use` permission. Every built-in role holds it ([Roles and access](/guides/roles-and-access)). A role without it sees "Your role cannot use Ankra AI." and a **See roles** link for an organisation admin.

## Ask a question

Under **What would you like to do?**, type into the box (placeholder "Ask anything. @ an app or cluster, / a tool") and send. Type `@` to name an application or cluster, or `/` to pick a tool.

The line inside the box says what the current mode can do: "Nothing changes from here." in Ask mode, "Nothing happens until you approve it." in Agent mode and "Nothing runs until you approve the plan." in Plan mode. The modes, and what Ankra AI reads, are on [AI Assistant](/platform/ai-assistant).

Below the box, three buttons fill it with a question to start from. They add to what you have typed and send nothing on their own:

| Button | The question it fills in |
|---|---|
| **Check my apps** | Are my apps running as they should? What needs my attention? |
| **Find savings** | Where could I save on costs without affecting my production apps? |
| **Catch me up** | What changed in my workspace this week, and what should I look at next? |

**Check:** the answer appears above the box, and the conversation is saved under **Conversations**.

## See what needs you

When something waits on you, a **Needs you** section appears below the starting questions. It shows the first four things the **Needs you** page lists, in the page's order and under its headings, **Act today**, **Act this week** and **When you can**, each with its one button. Home counts as the menu's **Needs you** row counts: only what your role can act on, and nothing until every place it looks has answered.

After them, **Needs you** lists a setup that waits on you, each with one button:

| Row | Button |
|---|---|
| An app still being set up | **Carry on**, back to its setup |
| An app whose setup stopped | **See what stopped it** |
| A playground Ankra deletes when its free day ends | **Keep my playground** |
| Your organisation's own cluster, when its agent has not connected yet | **Carry on**, back to the install step |
| Your organisation's own cluster, when its agent is offline | **Open**, the cluster's page |

When more than four wait, or Ankra could not read everything, **See all** *number* **in Needs you** opens the **Needs you** page (`/next/needs-you`); otherwise the link reads **Open Needs you**. That page lists every approval, fix and alert that waits on you in one order, by harm: see [Needs you](/platform/find-your-way-around#needs-you). When nothing waits, its title reads **Nothing needs you right now**.

<Note>
No **Needs you** section on Home does not always mean nothing waits on you: Home shows none while Ankra is still checking, or when a place could not be checked and nothing else waits. The **Needs you** page says which.
</Note>

## Go back to a conversation

**Recent**, under the starting questions, lists your three latest conversations. A dot before a title marks a new answer, and "Answering…" means Ankra AI is still working on one. **View all**, or **Conversations** at the top of the page, opens **Your conversations**, where you can:

- start a **New conversation**,
- find one with **Search conversations**,
- delete one, after the question **Delete this conversation?**

**Canvas**, at the top of the page, opens what Ankra AI made in the conversation you have open.

## Onboarding home

Home also shows **In &lt;organisation&gt;**, naming what runs in the organisation and what has access, and **Starting points**, the ways in from [the first screen](/get-started/start-here) the organisation has not used. An organisation that runs nothing sees the starting points as a list, each with a sentence saying what it sets up.
