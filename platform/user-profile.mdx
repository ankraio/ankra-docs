Some settings belong to you rather than to an organisation: where Ankra emails you, how you sign in, the accounts you connect, how you are notified, the tokens your scripts and editor use, and how the portal looks. They follow you across every organisation you belong to.

## Open your account

Click your name at the foot of the sidebar to open the account menu. It holds:

- **Profile**, which opens your account pages (`/next/account`).
- **Appearance**: **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Show technical details**, which opens every Details section and shows identifiers and exact values.
- **Support** and **Sign out**.

Your account has eight pages, listed in its menu: **Profile**, **Organisations**, **Invitations**, **Sign-in and security**, **Connected accounts**, **Notifications**, **API access** and **Preferences**.

## Profile

**Profile** (`/next/account`) has these sections:

- **About you**: your name, email and picture. They come from the way you sign in; change them there, and Ankra shows the new ones the next time you sign in.
- **Contact email**: where Ankra sends alerts and invoices, when your sign-in address is not the inbox you read. Enter the address, then the 6-digit code Ankra mails to it. The address is used once you enter the code.
- **Language**: the language the portal is shown in.
- **Delete your account**: removes your sign-in, your tokens and your connected GitHub account, and takes you out of every organisation. Clusters and apps stay with their organisations. If you are the only owner of an organisation other people use, Ankra asks you to give someone else the owner role first. Click **Delete account**, type your email to confirm, then click **Delete my account**.

<Warning>
Deleting your account cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Organisations

**Organisations** (`/next/account/organisations`) lists every organisation you belong to and your role in each. The one you are in is marked **You are here** and links to its **Organisation settings**; **Switch** moves you to another. When the list is long, a search field finds an organisation by name or ID.

To create one, click **New organisation**, enter a **Name** and click **Create organisation**. You become its owner and Ankra opens it; your other organisations stay as they are.

<CliVersion since="0.1.122" />

The CLI does the same with `ankra org list`, `ankra org switch <organisation>` and `ankra org create <name>`: see [ankra org](/reference/cli/org).

## Invitations

**Invitations** (`/next/account/invitations`) lists the organisations that invited you. **Join** takes you in. **Decline** asks **Decline the invitation to `<organisation>`?**; the invitation is removed, and to join later someone in that organisation has to invite you again.

## Sign-in and security

**Sign-in and security** (`/next/account/security`) holds **Ways to sign in**, **Two-step sign-in** (passkeys and an authenticator app) and **Recovery codes**. See [Account security](/platform/account-security).

## Connected accounts

**Connected accounts** (`/next/account/connections`) links your own GitHub or Bitbucket Cloud account (**Code**), your Slack or Microsoft Teams identity (**Chat**), and your own AI keys. See [Connected accounts](/platform/connected-accounts) and [Your own AI keys](/platform/personal-ai-keys).

## Notifications

**Notifications** (`/next/account/notifications`) sets how you are notified:

- **Email**: one switch per kind of email, sent to your contact email or, without one, the address you sign in with. The kinds that are always sent are listed under the switches.
- **Chat**: **Message me in Slack when an answer is ready** and **Message me in Teams when an answer is ready**, for when you ask Ankra something long. Link Slack or Microsoft Teams in **Connected accounts** first.
- **Phone and tablet**: the devices that get alerts on the lock screen from the Ankra app.

Which alerts go where for the whole organisation is set separately: see [Notification routing](/guides/notification-routing).

## API access

**API access** (`/next/account/api`) holds your personal tokens for the [CLI](/integrations/ankra-cli), scripts, CI and the [MCP server](/platform/mcp-server). A token acts as you: whoever has it can do what it allows, until it expires or you revoke it.

Ankra makes a token only for an account with two-step sign-in. Without it, the page shows **Add a second step to sign in first** with a link, **Set up two-step sign-in**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the token">
    Click **Create token**. Enter a **Name** that says where it will be used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it can do">
    Under **What can it do?** pick one:

    | Choice | For |
    |--------|-----|
    | **Editor tools: read and change** | Cursor and other MCP clients. Deploys, scales and applies, as you would. |
    | **Editor tools: read only** | Lists, describes and queries. It cannot change anything. |
    | **Full API access** | Scripts and CI that call Ankra's REST API. Everything you can do. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it expires">
    Under **Expires after** pick **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** (the default) or **1 year**. The field shows the date it stops working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the secret">
    Click **Create token**. The secret is masked until you reveal it and is not shown again after you close the dialog. The token now appears under **Your tokens**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Renew** on a token that is not revoked gives it a new secret, shown once, and says when the old one stops working; it is the main action on a token that expires soon. A token's options menu offers **Revoke** and **Revoke and remove**. **Connect your editor** gives the set-up for **Claude Code** and **Cursor**: Claude Code signs in on its own, so it needs no token. See [API tokens](/reference/tokens) for what each access level allows.

## Preferences

**Preferences** (`/next/account/preferences`) holds:

- **Appearance**: the **Theme**, saved on your account. The account menu's **Appearance** items set the same choice. **Show technical details** is here too.
- **Money**: **Show costs in** chooses the currency Ankra shows costs in. Invoices stay in your organisation's billing currency.
- **Ankra AI**: **When you ask Ankra to do something** sets the mode a new conversation starts in. **Ask** investigates and explains, and never changes what is running. **Plan** proposes a plan for you to approve before it changes anything. **Agent** does the work, and asks before each change to a cluster.
- **What Ankra may do without asking you**: the actions you approved once and told Ankra to repeat. Remove one and Ankra asks again next time.

See [AI Assistant](/platform/ai-assistant) for how the modes work in chat.
