When you sign in you want to know what broke overnight, what is waiting on you and which cluster to open first. The Dashboard answers that for the organisation you have selected, from top to bottom in that order.

<Note>
The Dashboard only shows clusters in the organisation you currently have selected. Switch organisations from the profile menu or the [command palette](/platform/command-palette) (**Switch Organisation**) to see a different fleet. In an organisation with [projects](/concepts/projects), several widgets cover only the project you act in: your [current project](/concepts/projects#your-current-project), which you switch in the new Ankra portal. A member limited to projects reads their own project only, also while the organisation has projects off.
</Note>

## Briefing

The line at the top summarises what changed since your last visit - for example operations that ran or failed, plans approved, AI sessions that completed and how cloud spend is pacing this month. Each sentence links to where it came from. When nothing needs you it says so. A new organisation also sees a setup checklist here.

## Your clusters

Quick links to the clusters you subscribe to, so the ones you work on every day are one click away. **Browse clusters** opens the full cluster list.

## Needs your attention

Approvals, failures and alerts waiting on you, in one place:

- **Inbox** - the same personal worklist as [Activity & Inbox](/platform/activity-inbox): plans to approve, actions to confirm, failed steps and AI sessions waiting for an answer.
- **Active alerts** - alerts firing across the fleet. See [Alerts](/guides/alerts).
- **Fleet issues** - offline clusters as critical issues, and failing operations, steps that failed and have not recovered, as warnings: one per cluster, plus one for platform operations outside your clusters, critical first. In an organisation with projects, it covers the project you act in. **Diagnose with AI** hands an issue to the [AI Assistant](/platform/ai-assistant) with its context attached.

## Fleet overview

Everything running across the organisation:

- **Fleet globe** - each cluster at its real location. Tower height follows node count and colour follows live status; select a cluster to open it. In an organisation with projects, it shows the project you act in.
- **Fleet pulse** - clusters online, failing resources, and cloud spend with the projected month-end total by provider and by top cluster. The full breakdown is in [Cloud Cost](/platform/cloud-cost). In an organisation with projects, failing resources and cloud spend cover the project you act in.
- **Fleet Security** - actionable critical and high findings, with the worst cluster. In an organisation with projects, it covers the [project you act in](/guides/cluster-security#which-project-a-read-covers). Opens the [Security Center](/guides/cluster-security).
- **Failure domains** - which clusters survive losing a zone and which lose quorum. In an organisation with projects, it covers the project you act in.
- **Fleet Backups** - backup posture and the worst cluster, when backups are enabled for your organisation.
- **Fleet utilisation** - the six clusters with the highest CPU use, each with its live CPU and memory bars; select one to open its metrics. Shown only when platform metrics are on for your organisation.

### Clusters missing from the globe

Ankra places a cluster from its nodes' `topology.kubernetes.io/region` label first, and otherwise from the public IP the agent reports, which gives an approximate location. A cluster with neither is listed separately instead of on the globe:

| Reason | Meaning | Fix |
|--------|---------|-----|
| `no_region_label` | No region label on the nodes and no public IP reported | Turn on public IP reporting on the agent with the Helm value `public_ip.reporting_enabled=true` - see [Cluster Agent](/concepts/cluster-agent#fleet-world-map-public-ip-reporting) |
| `unknown_region` | The region label is set, but Ankra has no coordinates for it | Nothing to fix - only the map pin is missing |

Draft and archived clusters never appear on the globe.
