<Note>
Ankra billing is based on **worker vCPU usage**. Each plan includes a free vCPU allowance - you only pay for usage above that threshold, billed monthly. [Playground environments](#playground-environments) are the exception: they are billed outside the vCPU metering. Pay-as-you-go organisations pay from [credit](#credit-and-payment-method), and saving a payment method unlocks €59 of welcome credit.
</Note>

## Plans

Ankra offers three standard plans. Enterprise is agreed with you by Ankra: the table shows its published rate, it needs a 12-month commitment, and it has its own support terms. An organisation on a plan agreed with Ankra sees **Your plan is agreed with Ankra** on its **Plan** panel and changes it through Ankra support.

| | Free | Startup | Enterprise |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Price** | €0 | €0.01/vCPU/hr | €0.03/vCPU/hr |
| **Annual price** | - | €0.008/vCPU/hr | €0.024/vCPU/hr |
| **Free vCPU allowance** | 30 | 30 | 30 |
| **Clusters** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Team members** | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **AI Assistant** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **SSO** | - | - | Yes |
| **Audit log export** ([CSV, JSON, API](/security/audit-export)) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Support** | Community | Priority email | Dedicated |

<Tip>
A 12-month commitment saves 20% on the per-vCPU rate. On Startup, **Change plan** offers **Billed**: **Monthly** or **12-month commitment**.
</Tip>

## Credit and payment method

Pay-as-you-go organisations pay for resources from credit. A payment method is required to use resources beyond the free playground and the monthly allowance, but credit is always spent first, and **you will never be charged without a top-up**: your card is only charged when you add credit.

### Welcome credit

For a pay-as-you-go organisation, saving a payment method unlocks **€59 of welcome credit, valid for 30 days**. The credit is no longer granted when the organisation is created - it is added when the card is saved, under **Settings → Plan and billing**. It is granted at most once per card and once per person: a card that already unlocked it for another organisation, or an owner who already received it there, does not unlock it again, and an organisation that already holds Ankra Cloud's sign-up credit does not receive it either. Credit you have not spent when the 30 days end expires. Welcome credit granted before the amount changed on 24 October 2026, or before the term changed on 13 November 2026, keeps its original amount and expiry date.

#### Sign-up credit on Ankra Cloud

Free credit on [Ankra Cloud](https://ankra.cloud) comes with the Ankra Platform. The Ankra Cloud account the Platform creates for your organisation, when one of its administrators first signs in to Ankra Cloud with Ankra or when the Platform sets up Ankra Cloud for it, receives **€59 of sign-up credit, valid for 30 days**. An account signed up for on Ankra Cloud directly, with an email and password or with Google, Microsoft or GitHub, receives no free credit when it is created. It receives the €59 once it starts being used through the Ankra Platform: when you connect it to your organisation, or when the Platform first works in it for you. The 30 days run from that moment, and each account receives the sign-up credit once. The organisation's credit, the welcome credit above included, pays for Ankra Cloud too, because the Platform and Ankra Cloud share one credit balance. Credit granted before 19 November 2026 keeps its amount and expiry date.

Until a payment method is saved, a new organisation can run its free [playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox), but cannot create or import other clusters. It can also spend a small allowance of pipeline minutes and AI each calendar month (UTC), and once that is spent its pipeline runs and AI pause until a payment method is saved. A free playground lasts 24 hours without a card.

### Top-ups

Add credit from **Settings → Plan and billing**, view **Credit**: click **Add credit**, enter the **Amount in euros** and, if you have one, a **Bonus code**, then click **Continue to Stripe**. You pay on Stripe's page, and the credit is added once Stripe confirms the payment. **Redeem a code** beside it adds credit from a code Ankra gave you; each code works once. Both buttons need permission to manage billing.

**Automatic top-up** is off unless you turn it on. Set **When the credit falls below (euros)**, **Add this much (euros)**, **At most this much a day (euros)** and **At most this much a month (euros)**, and Ankra charges your saved card when the credit falls below the amount you chose. We recommend turning them on for anything you need to keep running, so your services are never paused for lack of credit.

### When credit runs out

What happens when your balance reaches zero and nothing tops it up depends on what you are running:

- **On the Ankra platform, nothing that is running is stopped.** Creating new clusters and AI usage are paused until you top up. Your running clusters are unaffected.
- **On Ankra Cloud**, new resources are refused. For servers you created yourself, you are notified first, and after a grace period the running ones are stopped. They stay stopped until you top up. The server of a playground you keep, and the servers Ankra created for a managed Kubernetes cluster, are not stopped this way.

Adding credit restores access.

## Playground environments

A [playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox) is a small Kubernetes cluster that Ankra runs for you on Ankra Cloud, for trying Ankra, development and demos. Every playground starts as one server with 2 vCPU, 4 GB of memory and 20 GB of storage.

| | Free trial | Kept playground |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free. Nothing is charged when it ends either. | €4.99 a month, excluding VAT |
| **What it needs** | No card | An Ankra Cloud account that can pay, for example because a [card is saved](#credit-and-payment-method) |
| **How long it runs** | 24 hours. Ankra then deletes the playground and everything on it. | Until you remove it |
| **How many** | One at a time, and up to three in 30 days | One at a time |

How playground charges work:

- **By the second, never more than the monthly price.** A kept playground is billed as its Ankra Cloud server, by the second for as long as the server exists, and never more than the monthly price in a calendar month. Storage is included.
- **Paid from your credit.** It is billed as Ankra Cloud usage and paid from your [credit](#credit-and-payment-method). [When credit runs out](#when-credit-runs-out) says what that means for a playground.
- **Outside the vCPU metering.** A playground is never billed per vCPU, and it does not use any of your plan's free vCPU.
- **Keeping a free trial.** Save a card before the 24 hours end and Ankra keeps the same playground, with everything on it. It is billed from the next full hour.
- **No free trial when the account can already pay.** If your organisation's Ankra Cloud account can pay, for example because a card is saved, a new playground is billed from the start.
- **A larger size.** A kept playground can move to a larger server, at that server's price. The [Ankra Cloud price list](https://cloud.ankra.app/docs/billing) has the price of every size.

A playground ordered before playgrounds moved to Ankra Cloud keeps the size and the monthly price it was ordered at, billed by the second. Later price changes do not apply to it. Those sizes can no longer be ordered: a new playground is the one described above.

<Warning>
Playgrounds are for development and evaluation only: no production workloads, no personal data, no backups, and they are excluded from the SLA (Terms of Service clauses 6.2 to 6.4 and 9.9).
</Warning>

See the [Playground page](/guides/cluster-sandbox) for what a playground includes and how to start, resize and remove one.

## Plan and billing in the portal

Open **Settings → Plan and billing** (`/next/organisation/billing`). Its views are **Overview**, **Usage**, **Invoices**, **Ankra AI** and **Credit**. Anyone whose role holds `billing.read` sees the page; changing the plan, the payment details and the AI caps needs permission to manage billing, and without it the page says so at the top.

### Overview

The overview shows, from the top:

- **This month**: what the month will cost if your clusters stay as they are for the rest of it, how much has accrued **so far**, how much of the month has gone, and when it is invoiced. On Free it reads **No charge**; on a plan with a fixed monthly fee agreed with Ankra it shows that fee. When playgrounds are running, it says how much of the figure is theirs.
- **Plan**: the plan and how it is billed, its price, **Term** (when it renews) and **Payment** (**Card and details in Stripe** or **Not added yet**). Until a card is saved, anyone who may manage billing can add one with **Add a card**, which opens Stripe and brings you back here; on a plan agreed with Ankra no card is asked for. Once a card is saved, **Payment details** opens Stripe's customer portal, where you change the card and billing details.
- **vCPU, last 30 days**, with a link to **Cost by cluster**.
- **Recent invoices**, with **All invoices**.
- The **Ankra AI**, **Ankra Cloud** and **Image registry storage** panels.

While paid usage still waits for an owner, the overview also shows **Unlock paid usage**: an owner adds a payment method there with **Add payment method**, then approves paid usage with **Confirm charges and unlock**. Only an organisation owner can approve charges; adding the card needs only permission to manage billing.

The "so far" subtitle shows how much has accrued and what percentage of the month has elapsed.

### AI Usage and Free Allowance

The overview also meters your organisation's AI activity. Every AI request is tracked (tokens per model, priced in USD), and each plan includes a free monthly AI allowance that covers usage served on Ankra's platform key. The overview shows month-to-date AI usage, the allowance meter, and a self-serve **daily AI spend cap** (USD/day) you can set as a brake on total AI activity.

An organisation admitted to **the DevOps AI model by Ankra** (a limited set today; see [Models and Cost](/platform/ai-provider#the-devops-ai-model-by-ankra)) also has $50 a month on it, Ankra's own model, metered separately: the overview shows that allowance beside the ordinary one. Past it the model bills per token, and once the ordinary allowance or its credit is used up, AI features run on the DevOps AI model while it has room instead of stopping. Other organisations see no DevOps AI allowance.

See [Models and Cost](/platform/ai-provider#usage-and-the-free-allowance) for what the allowance covers, what pauses when it is exhausted, and how to keep unlimited AI with [your own OpenRouter key](/guides/openrouter-api-key).

## Usage of every meter

Worker vCPU is one of several things Ankra measures. [Usage](/platform/usage) reports every meter in one place - worker vCPU-hours, playgrounds, managed services, Ankra hosted logs and metrics, and AI - per month or day, split by cluster, application or managed service, with how completely each was measured and what it costs. Managed services are included during the pilot, and Ankra built-in metrics are included in every plan. Ankra hosted logs are included until 31 December 2026; from 1 January 2027 each organisation gets 5 GB a month free and pays EUR 0.25 for each GB above that, as [Ankra hosted logs](/guides/hosted-logs#price) explains.

## Showback by project

The invoice is one per organisation and never splits by [project](/concepts/projects). For charging teams back internally, **Settings → Plan and billing**, view **Usage**, has a **By project** section while projects are on: per project, the clusters it holds, their current worker vCPU, the worker vCPU-hours over the window and each project's share of the total, plus the hours no project could claim. The same figures are served at `GET /api/v1/org/billing/analytics/by-project?hours=720` and by `ankra org usage --group-by project`. Hours are attributed to the project a cluster was in when they were metered, so a cluster that moved mid-month is split between the two projects rather than rewritten. Nothing here changes what you are charged: it is a view of the one invoice, not a second one.

## Invoices

The **Invoices** view lists the organisation's invoices: one a month for the month before, and, for an organisation invoiced monthly that moves to Free during a month, one for that month so far once Ankra has reviewed and sent it. Each row has a PDF download and opens the invoice in Stripe: **Pay** on an unpaid invoice, **View** on a paid one. Unpaid invoices are marked **Open**. The overview's **Recent invoices** has the same actions.

## Ankra AI spend and caps

The **Ankra AI** view shows what Ankra AI cost across your organisation: **Today** against the **Daily spend cap**, the tokens used today, and **This month**. **Change the caps** sets:

- **Daily spend cap**: at the cap, Ankra AI stops answering until the next day. Spend on your own model keys counts too. Choose the plan's cap or **Your own cap**, in US dollars a day.
- **Soft token cap a day**: past it, unattended runs use the quick model for the rest of the day.
- **Hard token cap a day**: past it, new unattended runs are refused until the next day. It cannot be below the soft cap.

Empty token caps mean no cap. The caps count each day from midnight UTC. The CLI reads and sets the same caps; `none` clears one:

<CliVersion since="0.73.0" />

```bash
ankra org ai-spend-cap show
ankra org ai-spend-cap set --daily-usd 25
ankra org ai-spend-cap set --token-soft-cap 2000000 --token-hard-cap 5000000
```

## Credit

The **Credit** view holds the organisation's prepaid credit, which usage draws from hour by hour:

- **Credit**: the **Balance** and each credit line with what is left of it, plus **Add credit** and **Redeem a code** (see [Top-ups](#top-ups)).
- **Automatic top-up**: see [Top-ups](#top-ups).
- **Statement**: every change to the credit, newest first: credit added, usage taken each hour, and credit that expired.
- **Top-up receipts**: each time credit was bought with a card, with Stripe's receipt.
- **How you pay**: a company that cannot prepay can **Apply for monthly invoicing** with its legal name, registration number, VAT number, address and billing contact. Ankra checks the company's registration, VAT number and credit, and a person decides; until then you keep paying from prepaid credit.
- **Credit programmes**: credit Ankra gives to organisations that qualify. A person at Ankra reviews every application, and granted credit is used before credit you buy.

When you send either application, a notification under the bell confirms to you that it arrived, and Ankra emails you the same. When a person at Ankra approves or declines it, every member of the organisation gets a notification that says so, and the person who applied gets an email. See [Notifications and Needs you](/platform/activity-inbox).

```bash
ankra org billing wallet     # the prepaid credit balance
ankra org billing standing   # whether overdue invoices, the wallet or the free tier restrict the organisation
```

## Changing your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    In **Settings → Plan and billing**, click **Change plan**. The dialog lists **Free**, **Startup** and **Enterprise**, with **(now)** on your current plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    The dialog says what changes and when:
    - **Startup** from Free: an owner unlocks paid usage under **Unlock paid usage** on the overview. Nothing is charged before that.
    - **Enterprise**: Ankra agrees the price and terms with you, and nothing changes until you have agreed them.
    - **Free** from a paid plan: **Free starts at once, not at the end of the month**. Stripe stops billing your current plan. On a plan invoiced monthly, the month so far becomes an invoice that someone at Ankra reviews before it is sent, and a fixed monthly fee on your plan is charged to the end of the month, not in proportion; an organisation paying from credit gets no such invoice. If paid playgrounds are running, Ankra asks you to destroy them first.
    - On Startup, **Billed** switches between **Monthly** and **12-month commitment**. A running 12-month commitment shows its end date; Ankra support can end it early.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the dialog. Where Stripe needs your payment details, it asks you to confirm them first, and the change takes effect once you have. The **Plan** panel then shows the new plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Billing Is Calculated

Ankra tracks vCPU usage through periodic snapshots of your clusters:

1. **Snapshots** are taken hourly per cluster, recording worker vCPU and total vCPU.
2. **Billable vCPU** = worker vCPU minus the free allowance (per hour bucket, aggregated across clusters).
3. **Cost per hour** = billable vCPU × effective rate (plan rate adjusted for any annual discount).
4. **Monthly cost** = sum of hourly costs for the billing period.

Only **worker node vCPU** counts toward billing. Control plane nodes are not billed.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a worker vCPU?">
    Worker vCPU is the total vCPU count across all worker nodes in your clusters. Control plane / master nodes are excluded from billing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When does billing start?">
    Billing starts when your clusters report worker vCPU usage above the free allowance. If you stay within the free tier, there is no charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch plans mid-month?">
    Yes. Plan changes take effect immediately. Your invoice for the month will reflect the prorated usage under each plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do annual commitments work?">
    Annual billing locks in a 20% discount on the per-vCPU rate. You commit for 12 months and are billed monthly at the discounted rate. While it runs, the **Change plan** dialog shows its end date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I exceed the free allowance?">
    On Startup and Enterprise, usage above the free vCPU allowance is billed at your plan's per-vCPU-hour rate, and **This month** on the overview shows the estimate. Free bills nothing: the **Change plan** dialog describes it as a plan where staying over the allowance for long holds back new clusters and more nodes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
