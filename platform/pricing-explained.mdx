Ankra charges for the platform by the **worker vCPU-hour**: the CPU of the worker nodes in the clusters it manages, hour by hour, above a free allowance. The servers themselves are billed by whoever runs them. Ankra AI comes with a monthly allowance in every plan. This page explains how those pieces add up. [Billing](/platform/billing) has the plans, the credit and the invoices.

## What is free

| What | How much | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker vCPUs | The first 30 worker vCPUs of the organisation, every hour | Every plan |
| Ankra AI | A monthly allowance that grows with the plan. [Usage and the free allowance](/platform/ai-provider#usage-and-the-free-allowance) has the figures | Each plan |
| The DevOps AI model by Ankra | Its own USD 50 a month. Ankra AI answers with it once the plan's allowance is used up | Every plan |
| Welcome credit | EUR 59, for 30 days | Once, when the organisation saves a payment method. Once per card and once per person |
| Pipelines on your own cluster | Not charged | Everyone |
| Playgrounds | Not counted as worker vCPUs | Everyone |

An organisation **without a payment method** is on a smaller free tier. Each month it gets 100 minutes of pipeline steps that Ankra builds on its own build cluster and USD 1 of AI, on Ankra's model key and its own key together. It cannot create or connect clusters, apart from the free trial playground. [Why was I refused?](/guides/why-was-i-refused#the-free-allowance-without-a-payment-method) has the details.

## What is metered

**Worker vCPU-hours.** Every 15 minutes Ankra reads the CPU of each worker node in the organisation's clusters, both clusters you connected and clusters Ankra built. Control-plane nodes are not counted, and neither are playgrounds or archived clusters. Each hour, the vCPUs above the free 30 are charged at the plan's rate:

| Plan | Per worker vCPU-hour above 30 | With an annual commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Nothing, and the organisation stays within 30 vCPUs | - |
| Startup | EUR 0.01 | 20% less |
| Enterprise | EUR 0.03 | 20% less |

The Enterprise plan is agreed with Ankra and needs a 12-month commitment. [ankra.ai/pricing](https://ankra.ai/pricing) lists the published prices of every plan.

On the Free plan the organisation cannot grow past 30 vCPUs at all until an owner approves paid usage, which moves it to Startup. See [the 30-vCPU trial limit](/guides/why-was-i-refused#the-30-vcpu-trial-limit).

**Ankra AI above the allowance.** Once the plan's allowance is spent, an organisation with credit keeps using Ankra AI and the cost is taken from its credit. AI is counted in US dollars and converted to euros at the day's exchange rate when it is charged. You can also add your own model key under **Ankra AI → Models and provider**.

**Servers.** A cluster Ankra builds on your own cloud account runs on servers your provider bills you for directly. What Ankra Cloud servers cost shows under **Ankra Cloud** on the billing overview.

## Three worked examples

Each example is a small team for one 30-day month (720 hours), on the platform fee only. Server costs are your provider's.

| | 1. A playground | 2. A cluster you connect | 3. A cluster Ankra builds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **What runs** | The free trial playground | 3 workers of 4 vCPUs on your own cloud | 4 workers of 8 vCPUs on your cloud account, plus control-plane nodes |
| **Worker vCPUs** | Not counted | 12 | 32 |
| **Above the free 30** | - | 0 | 2 |
| **Plan** | Free, no card | Free, with a card (needed to connect) | Startup (paid usage approved, needed past 30 vCPUs) |
| **Platform fee for the month** | EUR 0 | EUR 0 | 2 vCPUs × 720 hours × EUR 0.01 = **EUR 14.40** |
| **Also** | Nothing to pay | EUR 59 welcome credit for 30 days | Your provider's bill for the servers |

In all three, Ankra AI within the plan's allowance and pipelines on your own cluster add nothing.

## Where to see it

Open **Settings → Plan and billing** (`/next/organisation/billing`). Its views are:

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Your **Plan**, **vCPU, last 30 days**, **Recent invoices**, **Ankra Cloud** and **Image registry storage**. **Unlock paid usage** shows the prices that apply to your organisation |
| **Usage** | Usage of every meter |
| **Invoices** | Your invoices |
| **Ankra AI** | AI spend against the allowance, and its caps |
| **Credit** | Your credit, top-ups and automatic top-up |

[Usage](/platform/usage) explains how a usage line reads.

## How to cap it

- **Stay within the free vCPUs.** Until an owner approves paid usage, the organisation cannot grow past 30 vCPUs, so the platform fee stays at nothing.
- **Cap automatic top-ups.** On **Credit**, **Automatic top-up** asks **When the credit falls below (euros)** and **Add this much (euros)**, with **At most this much a day (euros)** and **At most this much a month (euros)**.
- **Cap Ankra AI.** On **Ankra AI**, **Change the caps** sets a **Daily spend cap** (**Your own cap, in US dollars a day**), a **Soft token cap a day** and a **Hard token cap a day**.
- **Count your workers.** The fee follows worker vCPUs hour by hour, so fewer or smaller worker nodes cost less, and a deleted cluster adds nothing.
