Guided setup walks a new organisation from nothing to a website running on its own cluster, with a public address. This page describes it in the new Ankra portal, where it is the **Welcome to Ankra** wizard at `/next/onboarding` on platform.ankra.app. While your organisation can start the free cluster and has no cluster at all, the portal's home page opens it, from your first sign-in on, until you choose **I'll do this later**; someone an organisation has invited is shown the invitation first. In an organisation on the new portal, the classic address `/organisation/onboarding` forwards here. An organisation still on the classic portal gets the classic wizard at `/organisation/onboarding`, which still has four steps, a separate **Choose what to deploy** step before the website, and no starter gallery in the last one.

The three steps need the playground, which every organisation has unless Ankra has switched it off for yours. Without it, the home page does not send you to the wizard, and the same address shows **Add your first cluster** instead: **Create a new cluster**, where you choose where the cluster runs and how big it is and Ankra creates it and keeps it up to date, or **Connect a cluster you already run**, which connects a Kubernetes cluster you run anywhere by installing the Ankra agent in it. **I'll do this later** leaves the page. That page offers no free cluster and no website step.

There are three steps. Each one shows its progress and remembers where you got to, so you can leave and come back.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your cluster">
    One click creates your organisation's free cluster - a [playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox). Playgrounds are moving to Ankra Cloud dev clusters, a single-node Kubernetes cluster managed by Ankra; this is rolling out. The step shows the cluster provisioning, the domain your apps will be published under, and, for a free cluster, when it ends.

    If your organisation already has a playground, the step picks it up instead of creating a second one. You do not have to wait for the cluster: **Continue while it starts** opens the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unlock your credit">
    Click **Add payment method** to save a card. Saving it unlocks **€59 of credit, valid for 30 days**, which pays for your resources before anything is charged to the card.

    <Check>
    **You will never be charged without a top-up.** Your card is only charged when you add credit yourself. Automatic top-up stays off unless you turn it on.
    </Check>

    We recommend turning automatic top-up on in **Billing** once you are running something you care about: when your credit runs low it adds more, so your services keep running without a pause. See [Credit and payment method](/platform/billing#credit-and-payment-method) for how credit is spent.

    Cards are handled by Stripe; Ankra never sees your card number. You can skip this step and come back to it, but without a card a free Ankra Cloud dev cluster lasts 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your website">
    Pick a finished starter, or describe the website you want. Until you pick a starter, **Write it from my description** is selected.

    **Choose a starter.** There are 20 finished websites, from **Restaurant** and **Clinic** to **Portfolio**, **Wedding** and **Task board**. **Kind of website** narrows the list to one kind, such as Food & drink, Business or Personal. **Tell us about your business** is optional:

    - Leave it empty and the starter goes online as it is, without Ankra's AI.
    - Add your name, what you offer and where you are, and Ankra's AI fills in the starter's words, prices and sample data. The layout and your colours stay as they are. If the fill-in fails, the starter goes online as it is.

    **Write it from my description.** The box is then labelled **What is your website about?** and a description of a few words is required. Ankra first checks the description against the starters. When it uses words a starter is known by, such as restaurant, gym, plumber or app, the build starts from the best-matching starter and Ankra's AI fills it in. A description no starter fits, such as an invoice tool, is written from nothing by Ankra's AI.

    **Colours.** Keep **As designed** (each starter's own colours, the matched starter's included; a website written from nothing gets Ankra's default palette), or pick one of 12 colour themes, nine light and three dark. On a starter, a theme changes only the colours. Picking colours is instant and does not use Ankra's AI.

    **Where the code is kept.** You do not have to choose: Ankra keeps the website's code and deploys the site straight to your cluster as a Stack, and you can move it to your own repository later. To keep it in GitHub from the start, choose **Use my GitHub repository** under the description box. Pick a **GitHub connection**, the **Repository owner** (the GitHub account or organisation the new repository belongs to) and a **Repository name**, keep or clear **Make the repository private**, then choose **Use this repository**. Ankra creates the repository, commits the site to it and registers it as an [application](/concepts/applications), so every push is built and deployed to your cluster. This needs a [GitHub connection](/integrations/github); without one, the step links to **Connect your GitHub account**. **Let Ankra keep the code** goes back to the default.

    You do not have to wait for your cluster. While it is still starting, the step says so and the button reads **Launch when my cluster is ready**. Pressing it holds your starter, colours and description, shows **Your website launches as soon as your cluster is running**, and starts the build by itself once the cluster runs; keep the page open until the build starts. **Change my choice** gives you the form back as you left it. Once the cluster is running, the button reads **Launch my website** and the step says how long it takes: about a minute for a starter as it is, a couple of minutes for a starter with your details, and a few minutes for a website written from your description.

    Once the build starts, the step ticks off each stage, from preparing or writing the site to live, and you can leave the page while it builds. Once it is live, the step offers **Open my website**, **View the code** if you chose a repository, and **Go to my cluster**.

    If the build fails, the step keeps your starter, colours and description, so **Try again** needs no retyping. A build that started from a starter also offers **Launch the starter as it is**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you end up with

- A Kubernetes cluster managed by Ankra, with the Ankra agent installed and connected.
- A website served from that cluster at a public address under the cluster's domain.
- With a repository: an application whose builds and deploys run on every push, the same way as any other [application](/concepts/applications).

From here, everything in the [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart) works on the same cluster: deploy a Stack, browse live resources, and ask Ankra's AI about what is running.

## Good to know

- Filling in a starter and writing a website from a description use your organisation's AI allowance. A starter launched as it is uses none, so it launches even when the allowance is used up. One website is built at a time per organisation.
- Steps you cannot complete - for example adding a card without billing permissions - can be skipped. Ask a billing owner in your organisation to add the payment method.
- The free cluster is for development and evaluation. See [Playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox) for its limits and how long it lasts.
