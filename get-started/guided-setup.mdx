Guided setup takes a new organisation from nothing to its own app online on a Kubernetes cluster that Ankra runs, with builds, security checks, code review, pull request previews and database backups set up for it. If you have no code yet, it puts a ready-made website online instead. This page describes it in the new Ankra portal, where it is the **Welcome to Ankra** wizard at `/next/onboarding` on platform.ankra.app. While your organisation can start the free cluster and has no cluster at all, the portal's home page opens it, from your first sign-in on, until you choose **I'll do this later**; someone an organisation has invited is shown the invitation first. In an organisation on the new portal, the classic address `/organisation/onboarding` forwards here. An organisation still on the classic portal gets the classic wizard at `/organisation/onboarding`, which still has four steps, a separate **Choose what to deploy** step before the website, and no starter gallery in the last one.

The three steps need the playground, which every organisation has unless Ankra has switched it off for yours. Without it, the home page does not send you to the wizard, and the same address shows **Add your first cluster** instead: **Create a new cluster**, where you choose where the cluster runs and how big it is and Ankra creates it and keeps it up to date, or **Connect a cluster you already run**, which connects a Kubernetes cluster you run anywhere by installing the Ankra agent in it. **I'll do this later** leaves the page. That page offers no free cluster and no website step.

There are three steps. The page address holds the step you are on and what you chose, and once your app or website is going online the page finds it again when you come back. There is no cluster step: a block above every step shows how your cluster is doing.

**Your cluster.** Your app runs on a small Kubernetes cluster that Ankra creates for you on Ankra Cloud, a [playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox). Playgrounds are moving to Ankra Cloud dev clusters, a single-node Kubernetes cluster managed by Ankra; this is rolling out. When your organisation's cluster would be a free trial and it has no cluster yet, the page orders it by itself as soon as it opens. Otherwise, while there is no cluster to continue with, the block is headed **Create your cluster** and says what the cluster costs, and you press **Create my free cluster** (or **Create my cluster** when it is billed). When your organisation has used its free trial and has no payment method, the block offers **Open billing** and **Check again** instead. If your organisation already has a playground, the page picks it up instead of creating a second one. You do not wait for the cluster: **Your free cluster is starting** (or **Your cluster is starting**) shows above the steps until it runs, and your app goes online on it as soon as it is running. Once it runs, **Your free cluster is running** names the domain your apps are published under. If it stops, **Your free cluster is not running** offers **Order a new free cluster**: the cluster that stopped is removed first, nothing that ran on it is kept, and another is ordered. For a free cluster, the **What it costs** line in the second step says when the trial ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your app">
    Choose the GitHub repository your app is in. Ankra reads it and prepares how it is built and run.

    Without a GitHub connection, the step shows **Connect GitHub to add your app** with a **Connect GitHub** button: install the Ankra app on GitHub and choose which repositories it may read, and GitHub brings you back here to pick one. With a connection, pick it under **GitHub connection** when you have several, search under **Find your repository**, choose the repository and press **Continue**. **Give Ankra access to another repository** goes back to GitHub for one the Ankra app cannot read yet. For a repository with no default branch, **Continue** stays disabled until it has a first commit.

    No code yet? **Start with a ready-made website** puts a finished website online instead, and Ankra AI can fill it in from a sentence about your business. It skips **Choose its services**, and **Go live** shows the website builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its services">
    Tick the services your app uses: **PostgreSQL**, **Redis**, **MongoDB** or **MySQL**. Ankra reads your code to find the services it uses, so you can leave the boxes empty; what you tick is recorded with your app. Ankra creates a PostgreSQL database on your cluster when it finds PostgreSQL in your code. It does not create Redis, MongoDB or MySQL yet.

    **What Ankra sets up for you** lists what comes with the app:

    - **Built and deployed on every push.** Ankra Pipelines builds your app in your own cluster and deploys each change you push.
    - **Security checks on every build.** Your code, your configuration and your container image are scanned for known security problems, and a build with a serious finding is stopped.
    - **Ankra AI reviews your code.** Every pull request gets a review from Ankra AI that points out bugs and security issues.
    - **A preview of every pull request.** Each pull request gets its own copy of your app to try before you merge it.
    - **Backups of a PostgreSQL database.** When Ankra finds PostgreSQL in your code, it creates the database on your cluster and backs it up to Ankra Cloud storage, and you can restore it from Ankra.
    - **A secure web address.** Your app gets its own address with an HTTPS certificate that renews by itself.
    - **What it costs.** For a free trial cluster it says when the trial ends, that adding a card once your app is online keeps it running and gives you €59 of free credit, and that you are never charged without a top-up.

    **Put my app online** starts the setup. It needs a cluster that has been ordered and has not stopped, and a repository whose last setup did not fail; until then the reason shows under the button. Ankra itself waits for the cluster to be running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Ankra puts your app online on your cluster and works through the steps listed here. **Setting up your app** ticks off seven lines, in this order: read your repository and prepare how your app is built and run; set up Ankra Pipelines to build, test and scan it on every push; turn on Ankra AI code review for its pull requests; turn on a preview of every pull request; back up its PostgreSQL database, when Ankra finds one in your code; merge Ankra's setup into your repository once its checks pass; put it online on your cluster. A line Ankra cannot do for your organisation is marked skipped with the reason, for example when no staging cluster is set for previews, or when the app has no database to back up.

    Keep this page open: the setup only moves forward while this page is open, and it continues when you come back to it. After 30 minutes the page stops checking and says **The setup of your app is paused**; **Check again** continues it. Once your app is online, the step shows its address with **Open my app** and **Go to my app in Ankra**. Ankra cannot start a failed setup again yet; **Choose another repository** goes back to the first step. **Connect Slack** gets Ankra AI's reviews, deploys and alerts into your Slack workspace; an organisation admin can connect it.

    If you chose **Start with a ready-made website**, this step is the website builder instead: pick a finished website and its colours, and it goes online on your cluster in about a minute. Your website can be chosen once your cluster has been created. Pick a finished starter, or describe the website you want. Until you pick a starter, **Write it from my description** is selected.

    **Choose a starter.** There are 20 finished websites, from **Restaurant** and **Clinic** to **Portfolio**, **Wedding** and **Task board**. **Kind of website** narrows the list to one kind, such as Food & drink, Business or Personal. **Tell us about your business** is optional:

    - Leave it empty and the starter goes online as it is, without Ankra AI.
    - Add your name, what you offer and where you are, and Ankra AI fills in the starter's words, prices and sample data. The layout and your colours stay as they are. If the fill-in fails, the starter goes online as it is.

    **Write it from my description.** The box is then labelled **What is your website about?** and a description of a few words is required. Ankra first checks the description against the starters. When it uses words a starter is known by, such as restaurant, gym, plumber or app, the build starts from the best-matching starter and Ankra AI fills it in. A description no starter fits, such as an invoice tool, is written from nothing by Ankra AI.

    **Colours.** Keep **As designed** (each starter's own colours, the matched starter's included; a website written from nothing gets Ankra's default palette), or pick one of 12 colour themes, nine light and three dark. On a starter, a theme changes only the colours. Picking colours is instant and does not use Ankra AI.

    **Where the code is kept.** You do not have to choose: Ankra keeps the website's code and deploys the site straight to your cluster as a Stack, and you can move it to your own repository later. To keep it in GitHub from the start, choose **Use my GitHub repository** under the description box. Pick a **GitHub connection**, the **Repository owner** (the GitHub account or organisation the new repository belongs to) and a **Repository name**, keep or clear **Make the repository private**, then choose **Use this repository**. Ankra creates the repository, commits the site to it and registers it as an [application](/concepts/applications), so every push is built and deployed to your cluster. This needs a [GitHub connection](/integrations/github); without one, the step links to **Connect your GitHub account**. **Let Ankra keep the code** goes back to the default.

    You do not have to wait for your cluster. While it is still starting, the step says so and the button reads **Launch when my cluster is ready**. Pressing it holds your starter, colours and description, shows **Your website starts building as soon as your cluster is running**, and starts the build by itself once the cluster runs, from whichever step you are on; keep the page open until the build starts. **Change my choice** gives you the form back as you left it. Once the cluster is running, the button reads **Launch my website** and the step says how long it takes: about a minute for a starter as it is, a couple of minutes for a starter with your details, and a few minutes for a website written from your description.

    Once the build starts, the step ticks off each stage, from preparing or writing the site to live, and you can leave the page while it builds. Once it is live, the step offers **Open my website**, **View the code** if you chose a repository, and **Go to my cluster**.

    If the build fails, the step keeps your starter, colours and description, so **Try again** needs no retyping. A build that started from a starter also offers **Launch the starter as it is**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep it running

Once your app or website is online, a **Keep it running** section asks for a card. Click **Add payment method** to save one. Saving it unlocks **€59 of free credit** to spend on clusters, apps and AI for 30 days, and your free trial cluster keeps running once your card is saved.

<Check>
**You will never be charged without a top-up.** Your card is only charged when you add credit yourself. Automatic top-up stays off unless you turn it on.
</Check>

We recommend turning automatic top-up on in **Billing** once you are running something you care about: when your credit runs low it adds more, so your services keep running without a pause. See [Credit and payment method](/platform/billing#credit-and-payment-method) for how credit is spent.

Cards are handled by Stripe; Ankra never sees your card number. You can press **Skip for now** and add a card later in billing, but without a card a free Ankra Cloud dev cluster lasts 24 hours.

## What you end up with

- A Kubernetes cluster managed by Ankra, with the Ankra agent installed and connected.
- Your app, or a ready-made website, served from that cluster at its own HTTPS address under the cluster's domain.
- With a repository: an application whose builds and deploys run on every push, the same way as any other [application](/concepts/applications), with Ankra AI code review, a preview of every pull request and backups of its PostgreSQL database when it has one, where Ankra can set them up for your organisation.

From here, everything in the [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart) works on the same cluster: deploy a Stack, browse live resources, and ask Ankra AI about what is running.

## Good to know

- Filling in a starter and writing a website from a description use your organisation's AI allowance. A starter launched as it is uses none, so it launches even when the allowance is used up. One website is built at a time per organisation.
- Without permission to add a card, **Add payment method** is disabled and **Skip for now** lets you carry on; ask an organisation owner to add the payment method. Without permission to create clusters, the cluster block says so and asks you to have an organisation owner create it or grant you the `clusters.write` permission. A role without the `applications.read` permission cannot see an app's setup, and the page starts nothing.
- The free cluster is for development and evaluation. See [Playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox) for its limits and how long it lasts.
