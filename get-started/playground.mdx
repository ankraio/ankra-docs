A playground is a small Kubernetes cluster that Ankra runs for you. At the end of this guide you have one running, with a starter website online at a public address, and you know how to keep it. It takes two to four minutes and needs no card: the playground is free for 24 hours. [Playground](/guides/cluster-sandbox) explains what a playground is and what happens to it in detail.

## Before you start

- **An Ankra account with a confirmed email address.** After you sign up, Ankra says **We sent a link to verify your email address**. Open the link in that email to continue.
- **A role that may create clusters.** Without it, the playground page says **Your role cannot start a playground**: ask an organisation owner to start it, or to give you the `clusters.write` permission. See [Roles and access](/guides/roles-and-access).
- Nothing else. No cloud account, no card and no `kubectl`.

## Start a playground and put a website on it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the playground">
    <CliVersion since="0.10.0" />

    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Portal">
        On **Where do you want to start?** (`/next/onboarding`), under **Try a playground**, press **Start my playground**. When the playground is your organisation's free trial, Ankra starts it as soon as the page opens. When it would not be free, the page first shows **Start your playground** with what it costs, and starts it only when you press **Start my playground** there.

        You can also start one from **Infrastructure → Clusters**: press **Add a cluster**, then **Create free playground** under **Start with an Ankra playground**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="CLI">
        ```bash
        ankra cluster playground create
        ```

        For an organisation without a card this starts the free trial. Every playground starts at the same size: see [Changing its size](/guides/cluster-sandbox#changing-its-size).
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    **Check:** the heading reads **Your playground is starting**, and a bar moves through **Reserving a server**, **Starting your cluster** and **Connecting it**. In the CLI, `ankra cluster playground status <cluster-id>` shows the phase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a starter website">
    Under the bar are three finished websites: **Coffee & café**, **Portfolio** and **Launch page**. Press the one you want. You can change your mind until it starts going online.

    A website is optional. To go without one, press **Open it** on **Go straight to my cluster**, under the starters: the playground's own page opens, and the playground keeps starting.

    Keep the page open: the website you picked goes online by itself the moment the playground is ready.

    **Check:** the heading changes to **Putting** *the website's name* **online**, then to **Your website is live**, with the website's address. Press **Open it** to open it in a new tab, or **Copy address**.

    If the playground was ready before you picked one, the heading reads **Your playground is ready** and the starters are listed under it. A starter goes online in about a minute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look inside the cluster">
    Under **What to do next**, find **Look inside the cluster** and press **Open it**.

    **Check:** the playground's page in Ankra opens, and its connection reads **Connected**. It is also listed under **Infrastructure → Clusters**, by its name: `playground-` followed by six characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put a first add-on on it">
    The playground takes Helm charts and manifests like any other cluster. [Quickstart, step 2](/get-started/quickstart#2-deploy-your-first-stack) deploys Grafana to it as a Stack, in four steps.

    **Check:** a pod whose name starts with `grafana` shows **Running**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify

Open the website's address from a terminal:

```bash
curl -sI https://<your-website-address>
```

The first line of the answer reports status `200`. The headers under it include the security headers an endpoint scanner checks: `content-security-policy`, `x-content-type-options`, `x-frame-options`, `referrer-policy` and `permissions-policy`. A website put online before 27 November 2026 does not send them. On the first screen, **Try a playground** now reads **Running. Free until** *the time the free day ends*, and its button is **Open my playground**.

## After 24 hours

Without a card, the playground is free for 24 hours from when it started. The countdown shows on the playground's page as **Free until** *the time*, **then deleted.**

When the time is up, Ankra deletes the playground, its server and everything on it, and nothing is charged. While your organisation has a free trial left, a playground page still open on it starts a new playground straight away; otherwise it says **Your playground was removed** and offers **Start a new one**. Opened again from **Get started**, the page starts a new playground straight away while your organisation has a free trial left, and otherwise shows **Start your playground** with what it costs. A new playground starts empty.

## Keep your playground

To keep the playground and everything on it, save a card before its free day ends.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Keep panel">
    Press **Keep my playground**, next to the countdown on the playground page.

    **Check:** a panel headed **Keep** *the playground's name* **past tomorrow** (or **past its free day**) says what keeping it costs: **Paid from**, **Your card** and **When credit ends**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a card">
    Press **Save a card**. Stripe opens to take the card, then brings you back. **Not now** closes the panel and changes nothing.

    **Check:** the page says **Checking that your card was saved.**, then **Kept. Nothing was charged:** followed by the credit that pays for it. On the first screen, **Try a playground** reads **Running, and kept: your card is saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Working through an AI coding agent instead of the portal? Ask it for the playground's status: `get_playground_status` says when the free trial ends and returns `trial.keep_url`, which opens **Plan and billing**, where you save the card.

Saving a card charges nothing: for an organisation on pay as you go, which is how every new organisation starts, the card is charged only when you add credit. The first card a pay-as-you-go organisation saves unlocks €59 of [welcome credit](/platform/billing#welcome-credit), usable for 30 days and shared by everything you run on Ankra. What happens when credit runs out is on [When credit runs out](/platform/billing#when-credit-runs-out).

## What can go wrong

| What the page says | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| **Every playground is taken right now** | Every server that can run a playground is in use. Nothing was started and nothing is charged. Ankra keeps no place in line: press **Try again** in a few minutes. A website you pick meanwhile goes online once the playground is ready. |
| **No playground was started** | Ankra refused the order and says why under the heading, for example **Your organisation has used its free trial, so a new cluster is billed to its Ankra Cloud account.** |
| **Your playground did not start** | The reason shows under the heading. Press **Start a new one**: Ankra removes this one first. Nothing is charged for a free trial. |
| **Your role cannot start a playground** | Ask an organisation owner to start it, or to give you the `clusters.write` permission. |
| *The website's name* **did not go online** | The website failed to start on the playground. Pick a starter to try again. |
| **Ankra could not check whether a playground is free for this organisation, so nothing was ordered.** | Press **Try again**. |
