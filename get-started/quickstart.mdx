Each step below ends with something you can see, so you know it worked before you move on.

<Tip>
New organisation? Ankra opens on **Where do you want to start?** with four ways in. [Choose where to start](/get-started/start-here) compares them; this page follows **Try a playground**.
</Tip>

**What you need:** an Ankra account ([sign up at platform.ankra.app](https://platform.ankra.app), or from your terminal as below), and either a Kubernetes cluster you can run `helm` against, or nothing at all - the free playground gives you a cluster.

<Tip>
Prefer the terminal? One command installs the [Ankra CLI](/integrations/ankra-cli) and signs you up:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://ankra.ai/install | sh
```

Follow `ankra onboard` in the terminal to create your account or sign in. On Windows, run `irm https://ankra.ai/install.ps1 | iex` in PowerShell. Once you are signed in, the steps below work the same in the web app. An AI agent can do this for you: see [Sign up from an AI agent](/integrations/ankra-cli#sign-up-from-an-ai-agent).
</Tip>

This is the fastest single path through Ankra: a playground, one Stack on it, and a question to Ankra AI. To compare the other ways to begin (your AI agent, your own app, your own cluster), see [Choose where to start](/get-started/start-here).

## 1. Start a playground

A [playground](/get-started/playground) is a small Kubernetes cluster that Ankra runs for you. It needs no cloud account and no card, and it is free for 24 hours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Try a playground">
    A new account first confirms its email address: Ankra says **We sent a link to verify your email address**, and the link in that email takes you on. Ankra then asks **Where do you want to start?** (`/next/onboarding`). Under **Try a playground**, press **Start my playground**.

    The heading changes to **Your playground is starting**, and a bar moves through **Reserving a server**, **Starting your cluster** and **Connecting it**. It usually takes two to four minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put a starter website on it">
    Below the bar, pick one of the starter websites, for example **Coffee & café**. Keep the page open: the website you picked goes online by itself once the playground is ready.

    The heading changes to **Putting Coffee & café online**, then to **Your website is live**, with the website's address. Press **Open it** to see it in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the cluster">
    Under **What to do next**, find **Look inside the cluster** and press **Open it**. The playground's page in Ankra opens, and its connection reads **Connected**.

    Nothing of yours runs on it yet apart from the website. The next step deploys your first Stack to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Already run Kubernetes? [Connect your own cluster](/get-started/connect-your-cluster) instead, then come back to step 2. Want Ankra to build a new cluster on Hetzner, AWS, DigitalOcean or another provider? See [Add a cluster](/guides/import-cluster).
</Tip>

## 2. Deploy your first Stack

A **Stack** is what a cluster should run: Helm charts (called [add-ons](/concepts/addons)) and your own Kubernetes YAML ([manifests](/concepts/stacks#manifests)), deployed together in the right order. You will deploy [Grafana](https://grafana.com/oss/grafana/), the dashboarding tool, so there is something real to look at.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the Stack">
    On the cluster's page, open **Stacks** (under **Technical details**) and press **Create a stack**. Choose **Compose a stack**, type `dashboards` under **Stack name**, and press **Start the draft**.

    The Stack editor opens on an empty draft. Nothing is deployed yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the add-on">
    Press **Add**, then **Helm add-on**. In **Add a Helm add-on**, type `grafana` under **Find a chart** and choose the Grafana chart. Keep the **Pinned chart version** it offers and the values it starts from, and press **Add to draft**.

    Grafana now shows in the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy it">
    Press **Review changes**. Ankra asks **Create and deploy this stack?** Press **Create and deploy stack**.

    The draft ends once the platform accepts it, and Grafana starts deploying to the cluster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it is running">
    On the cluster's page, open **Kubernetes** (under **Technical details**) and choose **Pods** under **Workloads**. Within a minute or two a pod whose name starts with `grafana` shows **Running** in the `grafana` namespace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
You can build the same Stack by asking Ankra AI instead: press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd> on Windows and Linux), choose **Agent**, and type *"Add a Stack with Grafana to this cluster"*.
</Note>

---

## 3. Ask Ankra AI about it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask Ankra on the pod">
    Open the `grafana` pod from step 2 and press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>J</kbd>. The **Ask Ankra** panel opens. Choose **Ask** as the mode: it reads and explains, and changes nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    Try *"Is this pod healthy, and how do I open Grafana and log in?"* or *"What would happen if this pod restarted?"*. Ankra AI answers in the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Agent** and **Plan** are the modes that change things. [AI Assistant](/platform/ai-assistant) says what each mode may do.

---

## 4. Optional: keep the cluster's configuration in Git

Stacks work without Git. Connect a GitHub repository when you want every change to be reviewed, versioned and reversible: Ankra then commits each Stack change to the repository, and commits you push are deployed back to the cluster.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a repository">
    On the cluster's page, open **Settings → GitOps** and connect a repository. [GitOps](/concepts/gitops) explains each choice on that page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the commit">
    Your `dashboards` Stack appears in the repository under `clusters/<cluster-name>-<short-id>/stacks/dashboards/`, with one `values.yaml` for the Grafana add-on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## You are done

You now have:

- a cluster that Ankra runs, connected and showing **Connected**
- a Stack deployed to it, with its Grafana pod **Running**
- an answer from Ankra AI about your cluster

The playground is free for 24 hours and then deleted with everything on it. To keep it, save a card: [Keep your playground](/get-started/playground#keep-your-playground) says what that costs.
