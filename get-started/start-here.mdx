After you sign up and confirm your email address, Ankra opens on one question: **Where do you want to start?** (`/next/onboarding`). It offers four choices. Pick the one that matches what you have today; the others stay on Home under **Starting points**, so you can come back to them.

## The four choices

| Choice on the screen | What you end up with | How long | What you need first | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Connect my AI agent** | Ankra added to Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, the Claude app or another MCP client, and a first job done by it | About a minute | Your agent on a computer, and a terminal for Claude Code, Codex or the Ankra CLI | Connecting it asks for no card. A playground its first job starts is free for 24 hours. |
| **Try a playground** | A small Kubernetes cluster that Ankra runs for you, with a starter website online on it | 2 to 4 minutes | Nothing | Free for 24 hours, no card. Saving a card keeps it, paid from your credit. |
| **Put my app online** | Your app from a GitHub repository live at an HTTPS address, built again on every push | A few minutes | Your code in a GitHub repository | Free for 24 hours, no card: it runs on a playground. |
| **Connect my own cluster** | A Kubernetes cluster you already run, connected through the Ankra agent | About a minute | A payment method on the organisation, `helm`, and `kubectl` access to the cluster | Saving the card charges nothing, and the first 30 worker vCPUs are free. |

The figures in the table are the ones the first screen shows.

## Which one fits

- **You work in Claude Code, Cursor or Codex.** Connect your agent first. Its first job can start a playground for you, so you do not need a second choice to see something running.
- **You want to see Ankra work before you commit anything.** Try a playground. Nothing of yours is needed, and it goes away by itself after 24 hours.
- **You have an app in a GitHub repository.** Put your app online. Ankra adds its own setup files to the repository in a pull request and changes none of yours.
- **You already run Kubernetes.** Connect your own cluster. Ankra installs its agent, reads the cluster and deploys nothing until you ask.

Every choice leads to the same portal. A playground you start for one of them is the same playground for the others: an organisation has one at a time.

## Coming back to the first screen

- **Skip to Home** leaves the first screen. Once one of the choices is finished, the button reads **Go to Home**. Nothing is lost: Home lists the choices you have not finished under **Starting points**.
- When you come back to `/next/onboarding`, the question reads **What next?**, and each choice you started says where it stands, for example **Running. Free until** *the time* for a playground, or *your cluster* **is connected**. Its button then opens what you made.
- On a phone, **Connect my AI agent** and **Connect my own cluster** say **Needs your computer: email yourself the step**: they need a terminal, and the page offers to mail you a link that opens the same step on your computer. Ankra sends no such email itself.

## The fastest single path

The [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart) takes one path through all of it in about 15 minutes: a playground, a first Stack on it, and a question to Ankra AI.
